# Take Action

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**What can I do today that is not just posting online?**
:::

This is the movement's **DO** surface — the one section that answers *"I have read enough, what do I
actually go and do?"*

Everything upstream of it — laws, problems, politicians, decisions, evidence — is understanding. As the
specification puts it: a movement whose app can explain the problem in nine sections and cannot tell
you what to do on Saturday morning is a reading group.

## The tracks

**Challengers** · **Communications** · **Organizing** · **Lawmaking** · **Participation**

Grouping matters: a citizen who does one kind of work well should be visible as such, rather than
buried under people doing a different kind.

## What actions are worth

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| add a politician video | 5 |
| a mass communications post | 10 |
| add a politician channel | 15 |
| identify a challenger | 25 |
| a canvass shift | 40 |
| recruit a citizen | 50 |
| vet a challenger · hold a meeting | 100 |
| a media appearance | 150 |
| publish an explainer | 200 |
| recruit a challenger | 250 |
| a press pickup | 300 |
| a challenger files | 500 |
| **a challenger wins** | **5,000** |

**The ratios are the argument.** Adding a video is real work and it is not within a thousand of putting
a better person in a seat — and a scoring system that pretends otherwise will get you a great many
videos.

## Logging one

A title, a description, and **one or more evidence URLs**, added one at a time. Then a required
declaration and an attestation before Submit unlocks.

## The limit

**We only carry action that is legal and ethical.** The declaration has no preselected answer, because a
pre-checked "yes" is an answer the app gave on your behalf.

Choosing the other option does not throw you out — the submit row is replaced in place with *"then we
cannot take it"*, and nothing is saved.
