# Call your representative

The most common first action, and a good worked example of how the whole surface fits together.

## 1. Find out who they are

Enter your address or ZIP. The app resolves your districts and returns the people holding those seats.

## 2. Read the record before you call

This is the step that changes the call.

Open their record and look at the **acts**, not the award. Find something specific: a vote on an
instrument you care about, an authorship, or — most usefully — something they **blocked from
advancing**, since that is the part nobody ever asks them about.

## 3. Have one specific ask

Staff answering the phone log a topic and a position. What makes a call unusual is naming a **specific
instrument** and a **specific action**.

*"I am opposed to corruption"* is logged as nothing.

*"I am calling about the disclosure bill that was held in committee on [date]. I want to know why it was
held, and I am asking for it to be scheduled."* is a call the office has to have an answer to — and
one they will notice if it arrives twenty times.

## 4. Ask something that produces a record

The most valuable version of this call requests something in writing. An answer you can cite becomes a
source, and a source becomes a row in the movement's record.

A refusal to answer is also information, and is worth logging as what happened.

## 5. Log it

Title, description, and an evidence link — for a call, that might be a written response, a reference
number, or your own contemporaneous note. Declare that the action was legal and ethical, attest, and
submit.

## 6. What happens to it

It is reviewed, and if verified it scores — with its points **frozen at verification**, so a later
catalog change never reprices your work.

## Why the call matters even though one call is small

Because it is the only step in this whole product that reaches a person who can actually change the
outcome. Everything upstream of it is preparation for a moment where a citizen who has done the reading
asks a specific question that the office has to answer.
