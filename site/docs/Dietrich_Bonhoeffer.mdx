# Dietrich Bonhoeffers

The roster of modern Dietrich Bonhoeffers — ethics-first individuals who match the [Bonhoeffer Criteria](/docs/bonhoeffer-criteria) and who are willing to stand publicly against a captured system.

This list is not limited to politicians. It includes journalists, researchers, scientists, military and intelligence dissidents, citizens, and anyone else who meets the standard. Politicians specifically are tracked on the [Politician Challengers](/docs/politician-challengers) page.

## Why a List

A single Bonhoeffer can be neutralized. A thousand cannot. The purpose of this list is to identify, document, and connect the people who already meet the criteria — and to make the network visible to itself and to the public.

## Inclusion Standard

Every name added to this list must be evaluated against the [Bonhoeffer Criteria](/docs/bonhoeffer-criteria). Pattern of behavior matters more than any single statement. We are looking for a track record of speaking, persisting, and refusing to be silenced.
