# Record a position

## Start with the question

A decision begins with a question stated so that it can actually be answered. *"Is the economy rigged"*
is a topic. *"Does the settlement fund's non-disclosure provision breach the attributability
requirement"* is a question with an answer.

Sharpening the question is most of the work, and the app makes you do it first for that reason.

## Then your position, and your confidence

Both are recorded. Confidence is not decoration:

* It is what makes a later change **legible** rather than embarrassing. A position held at 0.6 that
  moves is somebody updating; a position held at 0.99 that moves is somebody who was overconfident, and
  both are worth knowing about yourself.
* It is what the **threshold** is compared against when the chain has gaps in it.

## Then the evidence chain

Each link is either something you **have** or something you are **missing**, and missing links carry a
reason: `classified`, `suppressed`, `blocked`, or `unknown`.

Links you have carry a source and a **strength** — how much this link actually contributes, which is
not the same as how much you like it.

You also set a **confidence threshold for this case**: the level at which you will act even though the
chain will never be complete. Stating it up front is the honest version; discovering it after you
already concluded is rationalisation.

→ [Evidence](../evidence/overview.mdx)

## Then the check, and the fix

The AI re-checks the reasoning from first principles, and its read is stored beside yours. Then you
cast the fix — what you would actually do.

## Numbering, and where it lives

Decisions are numbered within their area and stored under the area that owns them. The
**community → area → decision** hierarchy is how scope works throughout the product: facts, metaphysics
and law are all assembled along the same path.

## A partial decision is a valid decision

Almost every field has a sane default, so a half-built decision is always valid and always saved. You
can record a question and a hunch today and come back with the chain next week.

A product that demands a complete argument before it will store anything only ever receives arguments
people had already finished having.
