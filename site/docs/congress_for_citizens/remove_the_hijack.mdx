# Removing the hijack

Detection and exposure are preparation. This is the part that changes something, and it happens in
exactly one place.

## The ballot, and nothing else

Control over a seat is removed when more people vote for someone else. Not by a pressure campaign
that ends with the news cycle, not by waiting for an investigation to land, and not by anything
outside the law.

Saying this plainly is not a disclaimer. A movement that names capture and does not say how it
intends to remove it invites the reader to imagine something worse, and there is nothing here to
imagine. **The mechanism is a ballot.**

## Why the primary is the real deadline

By the general election the choice is already made. The work that decides a seat happens earlier, in
the party's own primary, where turnout is small and a coordinated base is worth many times its size:

1. **Start from the seat**, not from the scandal.
2. **Find who could hold it**, including people nobody has on a list yet —
   **[Good New Leaders](../new_politicians/overview.mdx)**.
3. **Assess them against published criteria**, before anyone has picked a favourite —
   **[Qualifications](../qualifications/overview.mdx)**.
4. **Converge on one**, because a [split field](/docs/replace_incumbents/rally_behind_one_challenger/) is how a well-funded incumbent survives.
5. **Do it before the ballot is set.** After that there is nothing left to coordinate.

The full version of this is **[Replacing Captured Incumbents](../replace_incumbents/overview.mdx)**.

## Removing the arrangement, not only the person

A seat can change hands and the arrangement can stay exactly where it was. So removal has a second
half that runs on a different clock:

* **Repeal the statutes that made the capture legal** —
  **[Problem Laws](../problem_laws/overview.mdx)**.
* **Pass the language that closes the gap** — real bill text, numbered sections, published and
  waiting for a sponsor. **[Fixing the Law](../fix_laws/overview.mdx)** and
  **[Fix Bills](../good_bills/overview.mdx)**.
* **Keep a record of [who blocked it](/docs/good_laws/who_blocked_it/)** — an obstruction is a decision, and it belongs in the record
  next to the votes. **[Good Laws](../good_laws/overview.mdx)**.

## What a citizen does this week

Not everything here is a two-year project. **[Take Action](../take_action/overview.mdx)** is the
catalogue of things worth doing now — call a representative, add a source to a record, vet a
challenger, recruit one — each logged with a link so somebody else can check it.
