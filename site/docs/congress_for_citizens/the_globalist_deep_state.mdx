# A globalist deep state

Used loosely the phrase means nothing, and its looseness is convenient for the thing it names. Used
here it has a definition that can fail a test:

> **Decision-making power that is permanent, unelected, operates across national constituencies, and
> is not answerable to any electorate.**

Three properties, all of them checkable. Permanent — it is there before and after any administration.
Unelected — nobody voted it in and nobody can vote it out. Cross-national — its positions do not
change when one country's government does.

## The test

> **Does the policy survive changes of party that voters were told would change it?**

This is the whole discipline of this page, and it is a much harder claim to make than it sounds,
because it requires showing three things from the record and not two:

1. Voters were told a change of control would change this. Campaign material, floor statements, party
   platforms — sourced, dated.
2. Control changed.
3. The policy did not.

One instance is an anecdote. A pattern across administrations, across parties, on the same policy, is
a finding about where the decision is actually being made.

## What citizens already noticed

The people this site is written for did not need to be handed this argument. Both party bases have
watched a position they voted against outlast the election that was supposed to end it, and both have
drawn the obvious conclusion — that the choice they were offered was not the choice that mattered.
This area exists to turn that conclusion into something a stranger can check, rather than something
each side only believes about the other side's presidents.

## What it is not

* **Not a claim that every long-serving official is captured.** Tenure is not evidence.
* **Not a substitute for naming the instrument.** "The deep state did it" is not a finding. Which
  policy, over which administrations, against which stated promise.
* **Not a claim about any living person** unless the public record carries it, attributed to its
  source. See **[Exposing the Deep State](../deep_state/overview.mdx)**, which holds the documented
  files and the strictest rules on the site.
