# Exposing the hijack

A finding that lives in a folder is not a finding. Exposure is a separate job from detection, it has
its own standard, and most of that standard is about making the work **attackable**.

## What gets published, every time

* **The claim, stated narrowly enough to be wrong.** Which force, over which decision, in which
  period.
* **Every source, linked at the door.** The roll call, the filing, the contract, the court record —
  the primary document, not a story about the primary document.
* **The arithmetic, in full.** Not a number handed down. The inputs, the operation, and enough detail
  that a reader can redo it with a calculator and get the same answer.
* **The gaps, named.** Where the record has been withheld, that is written down as its own fact
  rather than smoothed over. See **[Naming the gaps](../evidence/naming_the_gaps.mdx)**.
* **How much we know.** A knowledge figure sits beside the result so nobody over-reads a finding
  built on thin coverage.

## The standard it has to survive

> **A hostile, competent reader with the same sources reaches the same result — or shows us where we
> are wrong, in public, and we publish that.**

That is a higher bar than persuading a sympathetic audience, and it is the only bar worth anything.
A finding that can only survive among people who already agree is a press release.

## The rules about people

Exposure is where the risk of getting it wrong is concentrated, so the restraints are hard rules and
not preferences:

* **No claim of a crime by a living person unless a court established it.** Everything short of that
  is attributed — *according to*, *alleged in*, *reported by* — with the source named.
* **Arithmetic over the public record is not defamation**, and we do not flinch from publishing it.
  The two rules sit side by side without conflict: compute freely from what is on the record, assert
  nothing that is not on it.
* **A record is never quietly edited.** Corrections are published as corrections.

## Where it is published

Into the public repository, not into a database you are asked to trust — clone it and check any claim
without our permission. See **[Open Data](../open_data/overview.mdx)** and
**[Repos](../repos/overview.mdx)**.
