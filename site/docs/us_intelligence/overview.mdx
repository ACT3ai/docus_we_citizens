# US Intelligence

<CTA />

A country needs intelligence services. What it cannot survive is intelligence services that operate
**on the electorate rather than for it**. This area is about the line between those two things, and
about making the line checkable rather than promised.

## No removing democracy at home

An election is only real if the people running the machinery of the state accept its result and do
not work to shape it in advance. The specific concerns, each of which has to be testable:

* Collection turned on domestic political activity rather than on foreign threats.
* Classification used to decide **what voters are permitted to know before voting**.
* Investigative authority applied on a schedule that tracks an election calendar.

## No puppet master over US politics

The sharper version of the same problem: an agency that can determine what a member of Congress
knows, and when, holds a lever over the vote that no constituent has. Where control over a decision
runs through the information available to the decider, **the party controlling the information is a
party to the decision**. That is the test used throughout
**[Congress Working For Citizens](../congress_for_citizens/overview.mdx)**.

## The capture record on the agencies

Allegations that an agency has been controlled — or has acted as controller — are treated the same
way as everything else on this site: filed as named problems, sourced to the public record, with the
missing pieces marked as missing. They live in **[Problems](../problems/overview.mdx)** and can be
checked from **[Open Data](../open_data/overview.mdx)**.

## Whistleblower protection

None of the above is discoverable from outside without people inside who can speak. So protection is
not a courtesy here, it is load-bearing:

* An employee must be able to say that something is wrong **and make the specifics public**, not only
  report them into the chain that is the subject of the report.
* Protection has to survive the career, not just the news cycle.
* Retaliation has to carry a consequence, or the protection is decorative.

## No citizen data handed to foreign countries

Data collected on Americans stays under American law and out of foreign hands. A service that hands
citizen data abroad has converted a domestic authority into a foreign one — and the citizen whose
data it is never agreed to that. The outbound half of this problem is covered in
**[Foreign Intelligence](../foreign_intelligence/overview.mdx)**.

## What is not in this area

Foreign services operating on the United States — that is
**[Foreign Intelligence](../foreign_intelligence/overview.mdx)**.
