# No puppet master over US politics

The sharper version of the problem, and the one that needs no wrongdoing by anybody to be true.

## The lever

A member of Congress votes on matters whose facts are classified. An agency decides what that member
learns, when they learn it, and what they may repeat outside the room. That is a lever over the vote
that no constituent has and no election can reach.

State it as the general principle this whole site runs on:

> **Where control over a decision runs through the information available to the decider, the party
> controlling the information is a party to the decision.**

The same test appears in **[Congress Working For Citizens](../congress_for_citizens/overview.mdx)**
applied to money, and in **[Foreign Intelligence](../foreign_intelligence/overview.mdx)** applied to
an outside state. Here it is applied to our own agencies, which is the hardest place to apply it and
the most necessary.

## What makes it different from ordinary expertise

Every legislature depends on people who know more than it does. That is not capture. The difference
is **interest**: an expert who has no stake in the outcome informs a decision; a party with a stake
who also controls the inputs decides it while appearing to inform it.

So the question asked of any specific case is not *did they brief the member* but *did the briefer
have a position on the vote, and could the member check the briefing anywhere else?*

## How a claim here gets established

The same discipline as everywhere: which vote, which briefing, what was withheld, when it was
released, and who was better off for the timing. Filed as named problems with sources attached and
gaps marked as gaps — **[Problems](../problems/overview.mdx)**, cloneable from
**[Open Data](../open_data/overview.mdx)**.

Where a member's vote is at issue, the vote itself is public and feeds the voting-record awards —
**[Monkey](../monkey/overview.mdx)** and **[Llama](../llama/overview.mdx)**. What an agency told them
is context, and context never enters an award. See **[The Wall](../politicians/the_wall.mdx)**.
