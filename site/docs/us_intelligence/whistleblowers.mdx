# Whistleblowers

Everything else on this page's parent area depends on evidence that only exists inside. Protection is
therefore not a nice sentiment attached to the end of a policy. **It is the mechanism.**

## What real protection requires

* **Speak publicly, with specifics.** An employee must be able to say that something is wrong *and
  make the specifics public* — not only report them into the chain of command that is the subject of
  the report. A channel that routes a complaint to its own subject is not a channel.
* **Survive the career, not the news cycle.** Protection that lapses when attention moves on is
  protection for the period during which it was not needed.
* **Retaliation carries a consequence.** With no penalty attached to retaliating, the protection is
  decorative — and everyone inside knows which of the two is real.
* **Clearance is not a hostage.** Revoking access as an unappealable response to a disclosure
  converts a security instrument into a punishment.

## The classification trap

The material that proves wrongdoing is frequently classified *by the party the disclosure is about*.
That gives them a legal route to make the disclosure itself the offence, regardless of what it
revealed. Any protection that does not address this handles only the cases that did not need
handling.

## What this movement does with a disclosure

* Treats it like every other source: attributed, dated, and checked against the rest of the record.
* Publishes what can be published and **names the rest as a gap** rather than hinting —
  **[Naming the gaps](../evidence/naming_the_gaps.mdx)**.
* Keeps the person's identity out of it where they want it kept out. Anonymity is a safety feature
  throughout this product, not a loophole in it — see
  **[Trust Scores](../trust_scores/overview.mdx)**, where a fully pseudonymous citizen can still
  reach the top of the ladder.
* **Never records self-incrimination.** The product does not keep a file that could be used against
  the person who filed it.

## Where the law has to change
