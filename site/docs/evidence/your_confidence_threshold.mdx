# Your confidence threshold

## What it is

A number you set **per case**, before you finish reasoning: the confidence at which you will act even
though the chain will never be complete.

*"I will act on 85% certainty even if 15% is sealed."*

## Why it is stated in advance

Because the alternative is what everybody actually does: look at the evidence, reach a conclusion, and
then decide — unconsciously — that the standard of proof was whatever the evidence happened to meet.

That is rationalisation, and being right does not protect you from it. Writing the threshold down first
turns it into a position you can be held to, including by yourself in a year.

## Why it is yours and not ours

The movement does not set a universal threshold, and it would be wrong to.

The right threshold depends on **what the decision costs if you are wrong** — and that varies
enormously. Deciding how to vote is not the same act as publicly accusing a named person, and the same
chain can be sufficient for one and nowhere near sufficient for the other.

So: your threshold, per case, stated, and visible to anyone reading your chain.

## What it is not

**Not permission to conclude anything.** The chain still has to reach the threshold. A threshold of 0.3
does not make a weak chain strong; it makes your standard visible, and readers will judge it — which is
correct.

**Not a probability in the strict sense.** It is a stated bar against a computed reading of your own
chain. The value is in it being explicit and comparable across your own cases, not in it being a
calibrated frequency.

**Not a way around sourcing.** The movement's publishing rules are unchanged by anybody's personal
threshold: no claim about a living person without a source, ever, whatever you have concluded
privately.

## The honest framing

This pillar does not manufacture certainty and does not pretend to. It makes the **shape** of the
uncertainty explicit — what is known, what is missing, why it is missing, and what standard is being
applied — so that a decision taken under it can be examined rather than merely asserted.

That is a smaller claim than proof. It is also the only one available when the missing links are held
by the people the conclusion is about.
