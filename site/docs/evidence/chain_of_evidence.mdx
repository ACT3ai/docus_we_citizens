# Chain of evidence

## The case

Every chain begins with a **claim** stated precisely enough to be wrong, and a **threshold** — the
confidence at which you would act on it.

Stating the threshold first is the discipline. Deciding what would convince you *after* you have looked
at the evidence is how everybody rationalises, including people who are right.

## The links

Each link is one step in the argument, and carries:

**`statement`** — what this link asserts. One claim, not a paragraph.

**`status`** — `have` or `missing`.

**`source`** — for links you have. Something a stranger can check.

**`strength`** — 0 to 1: how much this link actually contributes to the chain.

**`reason`** — for links you are missing: `classified`, `suppressed`, `blocked`, or `unknown`.

**`note`** — anything a reader needs to interpret it.

## Strength is not enthusiasm

The field people get wrong. Strength is *how much work this link does in the argument*, not how much
you like it or how well sourced it is.

A perfectly sourced link that everybody already agrees on has low strength — it is not what the
conclusion turns on. A weaker link that is the **only** thing distinguishing your hypothesis from the
alternative has high strength, and if it is the one that is missing, that is the most important fact
about your chain.

## The conclusion

Computed from the strength of what you have, weighed against the explained weight of what is missing.
It reports whether a conclusion is **reached**, at what confidence, and a summary in the form the
charter asks for: *here is what we know, here is what is missing, here is the conclusion.*

## A half-built chain is valid

Everything except the case id defaults. You can write the claim and two links today and come back. A
tool that demands a finished argument only ever receives arguments people had already finished having.

## Two homes, one shape

The same structure is used as a standalone case and as the evidence block **inside a decision**. Same
fields, same arithmetic, same rendering. A chain you built standalone can be the chain a decision rests
on without being rewritten.
