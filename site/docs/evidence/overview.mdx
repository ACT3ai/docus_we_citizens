# Evidence

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**What if the proof is deliberately withheld?**
:::

This is the intellectual core of the product. Everything else is downstream of being allowed to reach a
conclusion at all.

## The deadlock this breaks

1. Powerful actors do something wrong.
2. A slice of the information is **classified**, sealed, or withheld — a gap that did not occur
   naturally.
3. Anyone who could recover it is **blocked**.
4. Citizens holding 95% of the chain are **labelled** for saying so.
5. The standard is set at **100%**.
6. The missing links are **the ones being withheld**, by the people the conclusion is about.

Permanent deadlock — and not by accident. It is the most efficient defence available, because it
requires the accused to do nothing but wait.

## What the app does instead

A **chain of links**, each either something you **have** or something you are **missing** — and every
missing link carries a **reason**:

```yaml
- id: l1
  statement: "The duty to produce exists in statute."
  status: have
  source: "https://…"
  strength: 0.9
- id: l2
  statement: "The internal decision to withhold, and its stated basis."
  status: missing
  reason: classified        # classified | suppressed | blocked | unknown
  strength: 0.0
```

Plus **your threshold** — the confidence at which you will act anyway — and a computed conclusion that
states what is known, what is missing, and what follows.

## Why the reason matters so much

`classified` is not `unknown`. A gap somebody created is a different object from a gap nobody has
filled, and treating them identically is exactly the error the deadlock depends on.

## One shape, two homes

The identical structure is used standalone, as a case of its own, and **inside every decision**. The
reasoning does not change depending on where you happened to open it.
