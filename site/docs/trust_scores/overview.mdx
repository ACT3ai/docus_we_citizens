# Trust Scores

<CTA />

Any system that pools citizen decisions has one fatal weakness: whoever can manufacture people wins.
Bots are cheap, paid accounts are cheaper than lobbying, and a purchased crowd is indistinguishable
from a real one unless something distinguishes it.

A **trust score** is that something. It is how a citizen becomes a countable member of a mesh of real
people — and the mesh, not the individual number, is the thing doing the work.

## What a trust score is

* **A citizen-side score, about you, earned by you.** Unlike anything published about a politician —
  which is always [an award, never a score](/docs/politicians/award_not_a_score/) — this is a citizen earning standing for their own work.
* **Compatible with anonymity.** A fully pseudonymous citizen can reach the top rung. Trust here
  means *there is one real person behind this*, not *we know your name*.
* **Built from a mesh, not a database.** Standing comes from your position among other real citizens,
  which is why a farm of fresh accounts cannot manufacture it.

## What a trust score is not

* **Not a social credit score.** It cannot close your bank account, remove your access to anything,
  or follow you off this platform. The full comparison is on
  **[No Social Credit Score](../no_social_credit/overview.mdx)**.
* **Not merit, and not karma.** Merit weight is
  **[Meritocracy](../meritocracy/overview.mdx)**; logged citizen work is
  **[Karma](../karma/overview.mdx)**. Different ledgers, on purpose.
* **Not for sale.** Money buys nothing here, and that is written down as a rule so it cannot later be
  presented as an obvious optimisation.

## Built and voted on the party doors

Trust is built and voted on at the **party level** — WeCitizensR.com, WeCitizensD.com,
WeCitizensL.com and WeCitizensSocialism.com. That is where a community actually knows its own people
well enough to vouch for them, and where the voting happens.

## The read-only roll-up here

This [non-partisan site](/docs/non_partisan_site/overview/) carries a **roll-up stitched together from the party doors, and it is read
only**. You can see the shape of the mesh from here. You cannot vote on it from here.

That is deliberate. Voting on trust is an act inside a community you belong to. The `.io` door is
where the cross-party picture is visible; the door you belong to is where you act. See
**[One App, Four Doors](../four_doors/overview.mdx)**.

## The mesh, and what it defends against

A mesh of verified real citizens is what turns "anyone can post" into "someone real decided". It is
the working defense against the three things that otherwise decide a public conversation:

* **Bots** — volume with nobody behind it.
* **Intelligence services** — foreign or domestic, running accounts that look like constituents. See
  **[Foreign Intelligence](../foreign_intelligence/overview.mdx)** and
  **[US Intelligence](../us_intelligence/overview.mdx)**.
* **[Paid influencers](/docs/foreign_intelligence/paid_influence_operations/) and propagandists** — real people, purchased reach, presented as organic
  citizen opinion.

None of these are answered by censorship, and this site proposes none. They are answered by
**counting only real citizens**, which makes the purchased version worthless without silencing
anyone.

## What is not in this area

* **Citizen merit and citizen points** — those are
  **[Meritocracy](../meritocracy/overview.mdx)** and **[Karma](../karma/overview.mdx)**.
