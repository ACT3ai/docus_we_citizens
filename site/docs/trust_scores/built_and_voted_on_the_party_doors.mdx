# Built and voted on the party doors

Trust is built and voted on at the **party level** — WeCitizensR.com, WeCitizensD.com,
WeCitizensL.com and WeCitizensSocialism.com. Not on this door.

## Why there and not here

Because vouching only means something between people who have some way of knowing. A community that
works together — on the same seats, the same bills, the same local problems — accumulates the
evidence that makes a judgement about a member worth anything. A cross-party aggregate does not have
that; it has strangers assessing strangers, which is how a trust system becomes a popularity contest.

So the voting happens where the knowing happens.

## What a trust score answers, and what it never answers

* **Answers:** is there one real, believable person behind this account.
* **Never answers:** what that person believes, who they vote for, or whether anybody agrees with
  them.

That separation is a hard rule, and it is the reason the mechanism is safe to run at all. A trust
system that quietly encoded agreement would be a machine for removing dissent from a community, which
is the opposite of the thing being built.

## Anonymity goes all the way to the top

A fully pseudonymous citizen can reach the **top rung** of the ladder. Trust here means *there is one
real person behind this*, not *we know your name*. Anonymity is a first-class safety feature of this
product, not a loophole in it — some of the people this movement most needs cannot participate under
their own names, and a system that quietly excluded them would be excluding exactly the wrong people.

## Not merit, and not karma

Three different ledgers, kept apart on purpose:

* **Trust** — is this a real, believable citizen. This area.
* **[Meritocracy](../meritocracy/overview.mdx)** — how much weight a citizen's assessment carries in
  a decision.
* **[Karma](../karma/overview.mdx)** — recognition for logged work actually done.

Collapsing any two of them would let standing in one buy standing in another, which is the failure
mode every reputation system eventually finds.
