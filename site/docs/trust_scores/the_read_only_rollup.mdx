# The read-only roll-up

The `.io` door carries a **roll-up stitched together from the four party doors, and it is read only.**

You can see the shape of the mesh from here. You cannot vote on it from here.

## Why read-only is the right answer

Voting on trust is an act inside a community you belong to. If this door accepted trust votes, three
things would follow immediately, all bad:

1. **Cross-party vouching becomes a weapon.** The cheapest way to damage another community's mesh
   would be to vote in it.
2. **The signal stops meaning anything.** A number assembled from people with no way of knowing is
   noise wearing a decimal point.
3. **This door stops being neutral ground.** The one thing the `.io` has that the party doors do not
   is that nobody is competing on it.

Read-only keeps all three from happening, at the cost of one feature nobody actually needed here.

## What the roll-up is for

* **Seeing the whole citizen base at once** — how large the verified mesh is across all four doors,
  which is the number that determines whether any cross-party decision is worth anything.
* **Cross-party decisions.** When the forum here reaches a decision, the weight behind it comes from
  verified citizens across every door. See **[The cross-party forum](../non_partisan_site/the_cross_party_forum.mdx)**.
* **Honesty about coverage.** Where a door's mesh is thin, the roll-up says so rather than presenting
  a confident aggregate over a small sample — the same coverage discipline used everywhere else on
  this site. See **[Evidence](../evidence/overview.mdx)**.
