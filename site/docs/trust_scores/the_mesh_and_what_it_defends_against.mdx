# The mesh, and what it defends against

Any system that pools citizen decisions has one fatal weakness: **whoever can manufacture people
wins.** Bots are cheap, purchased accounts are cheaper than lobbying, and a manufactured crowd is
indistinguishable from a real one unless something distinguishes it.

The mesh is that something. Not the individual number — the **mesh** is the thing doing the work.

## What a mesh is

Standing comes from your position among other verified real citizens, not from a row in a database.
That is why a farm of fresh accounts cannot manufacture it: each new account starts connected to
nothing, and connections have to be earned from people who are themselves connected.

The practical property: **the cost of faking standing scales with the size of the real mesh**, while
the cost of buying accounts stays flat. Past a certain size, the purchase stops being worth making.

## The three things it defends against

* **Bots.** Volume with nobody behind it. Cheapest to produce, easiest for a mesh to exclude.
* **Intelligence services**, foreign or domestic, running accounts that present as constituents. See
  **[Foreign Intelligence](../foreign_intelligence/overview.mdx)** and
  **[US Intelligence](../us_intelligence/overview.mdx)**.
* **Paid influencers and propagandists** — real people, purchased reach, presented as organic citizen
  opinion. See
  **[Paid influence operations](../foreign_intelligence/paid_influence_operations.mdx)**.

## None of this is censorship, and that is the point

This site proposes no removal of anybody's speech, and it would be the wrong project to run one: a
movement whose whole pitch is *check the record yourself* cannot also decide what you are allowed to
read.

What the mesh does is make **the purchased version worthless without silencing anyone.** Everybody
keeps talking; only real citizens are counted in the decisions. A budget that buys a hundred thousand
accounts buys a hundred thousand things that do not count, which is a much more durable defence than
a rule about content — rules about content have to be enforced by somebody, and whoever enforces them
becomes the next thing worth capturing.

## Not for sale

Money buys no standing in the mesh, and that is written down as a rule so it cannot later be
presented as an obvious optimisation. The moment trust is purchasable, everything above stops being
true in a single step.
