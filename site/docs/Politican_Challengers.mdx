# Politician Challengers

The roster of elected officials, candidates, and former officials who meet the [Bonhoeffer Criteria](/docs/bonhoeffer-criteria) — politicians who are willing to publicly name what is wrong and who refuse to be silenced.

This page is the politician-specific subset of the broader [Dietrich Bonhoeffers](/docs/dietrich-bonhoeffers) list. It exists because politicians sit at the leverage point. A captured Congress and a captured executive are how the system stays captured. Politicians who pass the criteria are the ones with both the platform and the legal authority to actually move things.

## What "Challenger" Means Here

A challenger is not defined by party. It is not defined by being in the opposition. It is defined by the willingness to publicly say, in plain language, that:

* The US government is being controlled by interests other than the American people
* US intelligence services have caused harm to American citizens — including, in some cases, deaths on American soil
* Foreign intelligence services operate on US soil and influence US politicians
* Many fellow politicians are compromised — by donors, blackmail, foreign interests, intelligence services, or party machinery — and serve those interests rather than their constituents

A politician who knows these things and will not say them does not qualify. A politician who says them once and then goes quiet does not qualify. The criterion is sustained, public, on-the-record speech under pressure.

## Inclusion Standard

Every name added must be evaluated against the [Bonhoeffer Criteria](/docs/bonhoeffer-criteria). Past words count, but recent and ongoing behavior counts more. Politicians who pass the test today and fold tomorrow get removed.
