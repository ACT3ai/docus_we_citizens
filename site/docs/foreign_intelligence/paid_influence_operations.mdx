# Paid influence operations

The cheapest operation available to [a foreign service](/docs/foreign_intelligence/overview/) is not a break-in. **It is a payroll.**

Accounts, personalities and outlets operating as though they were ordinary citizens, at a volume no
ordinary citizen can match. Most of it is not even illegal, and much of it is done by real people who
are simply being paid — which is what makes it so hard to answer with a rule about content.

## Why it works

Public opinion, as any platform measures it, is a count. Anything that can be counted can be
manufactured by whoever can afford the units. In a venue where a post is a post and an account is an
account, the side with a budget wins by arithmetic, and the citizens it drowns out never find out it
happened.

Both party bases have already noticed this about each other's online presence. They are both partly
right, and the useful conclusion is not about the other side — it is that **a venue that cannot count
real people is not measuring public opinion at all.**

## The defence, and what it is not

It is not censorship. This site proposes none, and would be the wrong project to run one: a movement
whose entire pitch is *check the record yourself* cannot also decide what you are allowed to read.

The defence is **counting only real citizens**:

* A **trust score** answers exactly one question — is there a real, believable person here — and
  never what that person believes.
* Verified citizens form a **mesh**. Standing comes from your position among other real citizens,
  which is why a farm of fresh accounts cannot manufacture it.
* **Anonymity survives.** A fully pseudonymous citizen can reach the top rung. *One real person* is
  the claim, not *we know your name*.
* **Money buys nothing here**, and that is written down as a rule so it cannot later be presented as
  an obvious optimisation.

The full mechanism is **[Trust Scores](../trust_scores/overview.mdx)** and
**[The mesh, and what it defends against](../trust_scores/the_mesh_and_what_it_defends_against.mdx)**.

## Domestic versions of the same thing

Nothing above requires a foreign budget. Paid domestic influence operates identically, and the same
defence handles it without a separate rule — which is the advantage of defending the count instead of
policing the content.
