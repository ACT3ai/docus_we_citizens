# Foreign Intelligence

<CTA />

A citizen's vote is supposed to be the last word on who governs them. It stops being the last word
the moment a service that answers to another country can shape the choice, the information, or the
count. Two jobs here: **prevent it**, and where the record shows it already happened, **expose it**.

## Removing democracy from the outside

The forms this takes are not exotic, and each one is testable:

* **Narrative control** — deciding what an electorate believes it is choosing between.
* **Compromise and leverage** over people who hold office or want to.
* **Access to classified systems**, which converts a foreign service into a participant in American
  decisions.

## Puppet-mastering politics and citizens

The same test used elsewhere on this site applies: where a decision depends on information or
leverage controlled by an outside party, **that party is a party to the decision**. When the outside
party is a foreign state, an American voter has been quietly removed from their own election.

## Systems, budgets and data

The structural half of the problem, where the exposure is written into arrangements rather than into
any one incident:

* **No integration of our military, classified systems, or data networks with foreign countries** —
  integration is access, and access does not un-happen.
* **No US defense and research budgets handed abroad** — American research money buying capability
  that ends up outside American control.
* **No citizen data to foreign countries** — the outbound leak that
  **[US Intelligence](../us_intelligence/overview.mdx)** covers from the domestic side.

## Paid influencers and propagandists

The cheapest available operation is not a break-in. It is a payroll — accounts, personalities and
outlets operating as if they were ordinary citizens, at a scale no ordinary citizen can match.

The defense is not censorship, and this site does not propose any. It is **a [mesh of verified](/docs/trust_scores/the_mesh_and_what_it_defends_against/) real
citizens whose votes count**, which is what makes a purchased crowd stop working. That mechanism is
**[Trust Scores](../trust_scores/overview.mdx)**.

## What is not in this area

* **Our own agencies** — that is **[US Intelligence](../us_intelligence/overview.mdx)**.
* **The mechanism that defends the vote against bots and paid influence** — that is
  **[Trust Scores](../trust_scores/overview.mdx)**.
