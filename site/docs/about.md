# About We The Citizens

We The Citizens is a lightweight, ethics-first political movement. It exists to carry
forward the work Charlie Kirk started — and to do it inside a structure that cannot be
quietly taken over.

## Why we exist

Charlie Kirk built the largest conservative youth organization in America. After his
death on September 10, 2025, that organization was restructured within days, in ways
supporters say served interests he had publicly opposed.

The lesson we took from that is not about any one person. It is about structure. An
organization whose leadership answers to donors, sponsors, or a self-appointing board
can be redirected the moment its founder is gone. An organization whose leadership
answers to its members cannot.

So We The Citizens is not a tribute. It is a correction.

## What we are

* **A movement, not a bureaucracy.** Very lightweight by design — the way Charlie Kirk
  always said TPUSA should have worked.
* **Member-owned.** Every major decision flows up from the membership. The board serves
  at the pleasure of the people, not the other way around.
* **Radically transparent.** All finances, all board votes, all major decisions —
  documented and published. Every dollar visible.
* **Uncapturable.** No single donor, corporation, or foreign interest may hold outsized
  influence. Grassroots funding from real Americans only.
* **Accountable.** An elected board with term limits, annual independent audits published
  in full, and any board member recallable by member vote.

## What guides us

Everything we do is measured against the [seven charter principles](/docs/intro) —
good ethics first, politicians who work for citizens, exposing the deep state,
Christian values, online political thought leadership, winning with less money, and
ending voter fraud permanently.

Read them the way they were written: as articles of a founding document.

## How we act

Two core programs carry the charter into the world:

* **The We The Citizens Conference** — a once-a-year national gathering of thought
  leaders from across the political spectrum, with speakers selected by the board.
* **Campus Debates** — great political thought leaders visiting universities, taking
  questions, and debating in the open, with the content repurposed to extend its reach.

## The 1,000 Bonhoeffers

Charlie Kirk spoke repeatedly about wanting to find and encourage
[1,000 Dietrich Bonhoeffers](/docs/dietrich-bonhoeffers). The reference is to the
German pastor who stood against the Nazi regime and was executed for it weeks before
the war in Europe ended — and the point is the character type, not the religion:
ethics first, willing to stand against a captured system at personal cost.

A single reformer can be removed and the work stops. A distributed network of a
thousand ethics-first leaders cannot be stopped that way. That is the standard we
recruit to.

## Who runs it

The [founding board](/docs/board) is made up of people who stood with Charlie Kirk —
not people who stood to profit from his absence. Every member is term-limited, subject
to member recall, required to disclose financial interests publicly, and bound by the
charter to serve member interests above all else.
