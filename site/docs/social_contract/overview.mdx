# The Social Contract

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Measured against WHAT? Who says you are right?**
:::

Every claim this movement makes is a claim about **misalignment** — that what is happening differs from
what was promised. A claim like that is worthless without a yardstick, and worse than worthless if the
yardstick is our own opinion.

## The standard

**For a US citizen: the Constitution, and representative democracy actually working as promised.**

Not our reading of what is good. Not a policy platform. The deal that already exists, the one nobody
has to be persuaded to accept, and the one the people we are measuring swore an oath to.

## Why that choice is doing so much work

Because it is **not ours**. We did not write it, we cannot amend it to win an argument, and it binds us
exactly as tightly as it binds anyone else.

It also means people who disagree violently about policy can use the same instrument. Whether a duty
was met, whether a check was removed, whether a vote was allowed to happen — these are structural
questions, and their answers do not depend on what you think the tax rate should be.

## It is per-country; the machinery is not

The reference standard plugs in per country. The loop — **record, reason from first principles, decide,
optionally publish** — is universal. A citizen anywhere can run this with their own contract in place.

## How the standard reaches the reasoning

Not as a slogan. The app assembles it as **layers**, from the most general to the most specific:

```
Earth -> country -> state / province -> community -> area -> decision
```

Two parallel libraries are built this way — **facts** (what we know) and **metaphysics** (what is real
and what the reasoning may assume) — plus the relevant law. Each starts **empty** and concatenates only
the layers that apply, every block headed with the file it came from.

Nothing enters the reasoning unless a layer explicitly put it there.
