# Good Laws

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Has anyone tried to fix this, and what happened?**
:::

Laws and bills that would repair democracy — the ones that passed, the ones voted down, and the ones never allowed a vote at all.

## What this area covers

- The good law list and its admission tests (repeal, restore, transparency, accountability, franchise, due process, speech, purse).
- The goodness rating and the citizen vote on it.
- The three fates of a good law.
- Who blocked one from advancing and how that is recorded..

## What it deliberately leaves to other areas

- The reward a politician earns for advancing them — that is llama..
