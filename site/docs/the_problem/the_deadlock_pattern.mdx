# The deadlock pattern

This is the single most important page in this area, because everything the movement built downstream
is a response to it.

## The pattern, step by step

1. **Powerful actors do something wrong.**
2. **A slice of the information is classified**, sealed, or withheld — creating a gap in the evidence
   chain that did not occur naturally.
3. **Anyone capable of recovering that slice is blocked** from doing so. Journalists are denied
   access, requests are refused, litigation runs longer than anyone can fund.
4. **Citizens who have most of the chain are labelled** for having the rest of it. Having 95% and
   saying so becomes the accusation.
5. **The standard is set at 100%** — you may not act, may not conclude, may not even say it out loud,
   until you have a complete chain.
6. **The missing links are the ones being withheld.** Deliberately. By the people the conclusion is
   about.

The result is permanent deadlock, and it is not an accident of the system. It is the most efficient
available defence, because it requires the accused to do nothing but wait.

## Why it works so well

Because each step is individually defensible.

Classification protects real sources. Evidentiary standards protect real people from real slander.
Caution about unproven claims is a genuine virtue. **Every element of the trap is a good principle,
which is why pointing at any single one of them gets you nowhere.**

It is the *composition* that is the abuse: the same actor controls both the missing evidence and the
standard of proof.

## What the movement does instead

It refuses the framing rather than the standards. Concretely, a citizen can:

* **Lay out every link they DO have**, each with a source and a strength.
* **Name every link that is MISSING**, and tag *why* — `classified`, `suppressed`, `blocked`, or
  `unknown`. A gap with a reason is a different object from ignorance.
* **Set their own threshold for acting.** *"I will act on 85% certainty even if 15% is sealed"* is a
  stated, inspectable position rather than a hidden one.
* **Have the logic checked from first principles** — not *did an authority endorse this*, but *given
  these premises, does this follow, and which link is bearing weight it cannot carry?*
* **Reach a conclusion with the whole chain visible**, gaps included, so anybody can see exactly what
  it rests on and exactly where it does not.

## The honest part

This does not manufacture certainty, and it is not supposed to. It makes the *shape* of the uncertainty
explicit, so a decision made under it can be examined rather than merely asserted.

That is [Pillar 4](../evidence/overview.mdx), and it is the intellectual core of the entire product.
