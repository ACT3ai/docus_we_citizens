# Democracy as promised

## The deal

Representative democracy is a delegation with a check on it:

1. You cannot personally decide every question, so you **elect somebody** to decide on your behalf.
2. They decide.
3. **You can see what they decided.**
4. If you do not like it, you **replace them**.

Steps 1, 2 and 4 are in reasonably good shape. Elections happen, representatives vote, incumbents
sometimes lose.

**Step 3 is the one that broke**, and it is the one everything else depends on. A delegation you cannot
audit is not a delegation. It is a transfer.

## What breaking step 3 looks like in practice

**Provisions arrive inside vehicles nobody can vote against.** A consequential change rides inside a
4,000-page must-pass package. The representative's yes vote is real and it is also not a vote on that
provision, and both of those facts are true at once.

**Decisions move to where there is no roll call.** Committee, rule, calendar, conference. Each of those
can end a bill, none of them produces a record you can look up, and the outcome is identical to a vote
against.

**The record is produced slowly enough not to matter.** A document released after the decision it would
have informed is not disclosure; it is archiving.

**The people who could compel the record are the people it is about.**

## Why this is not a partisan claim

Every mechanism above is available to whoever holds the gavel, and every one of them has been used by
whoever held it. That is precisely why the movement's yardstick is structural rather than political:
**a rule that only bites your opponents is not a rule, it is a tactic**, and it will be turned around
within one cycle.

The test the movement applies to itself, in exactly these words: *would this still be true if the
person it lands on had a different letter after their name?* If the answer is no, it does not ship.

## What repairing step 3 requires

A duty on a named person, a date, a consequence for missing it, and somebody outside the body being
checked who can verify it happened. Every fix bill in the movement's library is some arrangement of
those four things, because there is nothing else that works.
