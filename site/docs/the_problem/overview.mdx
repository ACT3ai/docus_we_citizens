# The Problem

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Why does anything need fixing?**
:::

Everything in this area is stated so that somebody who disagrees with us about policy can still check
whether it is true. If a claim here requires you to already share our conclusions, it is a bad claim
and we want to hear about it.

## The short version

Representative democracy makes a specific promise: **you elect people, they act on your behalf, and
you can see what they did and replace them if you do not like it.** Every part of that promise depends
on the last part — visibility. Remove the record and the whole mechanism stops working, while
continuing to look exactly the same from outside.

Most of what has gone wrong is not a dramatic seizure of power. It is the quiet removal of the ability
to check.

## Three things that are actually broken

**The record can be withheld indefinitely, with no clock and no penalty.** An agency that simply does
not answer faces nothing. Delay is a complete defence.

**The people who write the rules can exempt themselves from them.** And do.

**Decisions with the largest effect leave the least trace.** A floor vote is public and reported.
Keeping a bill from ever reaching the floor is one person deciding for everybody, in a room, with no
roll call — and it is functionally identical to defeating it.

## And one pattern that ties them together

Withhold a slice of the record. Block anyone from recovering it. Then insist that nobody may act until
they have 100% of a chain you deliberately broke.

That is [the deadlock pattern](./the_deadlock_pattern.mdx), and it is the thing the whole product is
built to break.
