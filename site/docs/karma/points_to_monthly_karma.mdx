# Points to monthly karma

## Layer 1 — raw points

Every action type in the catalog carries a point value, defined in exactly one place and published.
The spread is wide on purpose:

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| add a politician video | 5 |
| a mass communications post | 10 |
| add a politician channel | 15 |
| identify a challenger | 25 |
| flag a worrisome challenger | 25 |
| a canvass shift | 40 |
| recruit a citizen | 50 |
| vet a challenger | 100 |
| hold a meeting | 100 |
| a media appearance | 150 |
| publish an explainer | 200 |
| recruit a challenger | 250 |
| a press pickup | 300 |
| a challenger files | 500 |
| **a challenger wins** | **5,000** |

The ratios are the argument. Adding a video is real work and it is not within a thousand of putting a
better person in a seat, and a scoring system that pretends otherwise will get you a lot of videos.

Actions are grouped into five **tracks** — challengers, communications, organizing, lawmaking,
participation — so a citizen who does one kind of work well is visible as such rather than being buried
under people who do a different kind.

Points are **frozen at verification**. If a catalog value changes later, already-verified work keeps
what it was awarded. Nobody's past is silently repriced.

## Layer 2 — the normalized monthly score

At month close, raw points are summarised into a **normalized monthly karma score**, and everything
downstream uses that.

Normalization is what makes the number comparable — across citizens, across tracks, and across
communities of very different sizes. A month of organizing in a community of thirty and a month of
lawmaking in a community of three thousand should be readable side by side.

## The month is sealed

Once closed, a month is **sealed**. It is not recomputed when the catalog changes or when somebody
files something late. The record of what the movement recognised, when it recognised it, stays fixed —
the same discipline the sealed editions of every other calculation follow.
