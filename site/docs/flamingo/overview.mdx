# The Flamingo Award

The **Flamingo Award** is what a person is given for everything **outside** their in-office voting
record: transcripts of their own words, interviews, background, resume, education, public commitments,
and qualification assessments.

It is the only award most people on the [Good New Leaders](../new_politicians/overview.mdx) roster can
have, because most of them have never held office. It is also the only award of the three with a state
meaning **we have not looked yet** — and that state is not a finding against anybody.

## The three states

| State | Mark | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **earned** | the flamingo in full colour | assessed, an award was earned, published **with its percentage** |
| **not earned** | the same silhouette in solid **dark red** | assessed, the arithmetic ran, **no part** of the award was earned |
| **unknown** | the same silhouette in solid **gray-brown** | we do not have enough information, or have not run it — **this is not a bad result** |

## The rule that governs everything here

**The red and the gray-brown must never collapse into one another.**

They are the difference between *we considered you and gave you nothing* and *we have not considered
you*, and the second is not a claim about the person at all. Rendering them the same way — or letting
"unassessed" quietly sort with "failed" — would publish an accusation the movement never made.

This is why all three states share one silhouette and one pixel footprint, and differ only in fill. A
reader learns one shape and reads its colour, and no layout can accidentally turn an absence into a
finding.

## The wall

Nothing here touches the [Monkey](../monkey/overview.mdx) or the [Llama](../llama/overview.mdx). Those
are computed from the in-office voting record alone. Transcripts, interviews, background, resumes,
education and qualification assessments feed **this** award and never bubble up into those.

A person can hold both kinds at once — a voting-record award on one side, a Flamingo state on the other
— and they render side by side without either contaminating the other.
