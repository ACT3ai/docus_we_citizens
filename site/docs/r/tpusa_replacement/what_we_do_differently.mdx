# What We Take Over, and What We Do Differently

Same slot, different method, different output.

* **Same slot.** Organizing the political right, especially the part of it that is done waiting on the existing machinery.
* **Different method.** Candidates are surfaced by meritocracy and community voting, not chosen by a small group and announced downward.
* **Different output.** A package the community itself builds, not a message it receives.
