# Dedicated to Always Work for Citizens

The test is what happens after the election, not what was promised before it.

* The test is whether they keep working for citizens **after** they are elected, not what they promise before.
* Track record, not rhetoric. A speech is not evidence; a vote is.
* This is what the [trust scores](/docs/r/trust_scores) are measuring.
