# About Us for Republican Voters

This door is for you if you vote Republican. It is the room where the political right does its own work — finding challengers, writing the laws it cares about, and judging the people who ask for its votes.

* **Who it is for.** Republican voters, and the political right more broadly.
* **Who it is not for.** A visitor who wants the neutral version goes to [WeTheCitizens.io](/) instead. The .io is the lobby. This is the room where the work happens.
* **Why we exist.** To establish whether a member of Congress is captured — from their votes and their own recorded words — and to replace the ones who are with challengers who are not.
* **The promise.** Your side builds its own package here, in your own language. Nobody hands it down to you.

Everything on this door points at one thing: your elected officials, what they actually voted for, and who could replace them.

## In this area

* [Republican Voters, Not the DC Swamp](./republican_voters_not_the_swamp.mdx) — We are talking to the voter, not to the officeholder and not to the party apparatus.
* [Why You Are Here and Not on the Non-Partisan Site](./why_here_not_the_io.mdx) — The .io is deliberately neutral and will not take your partisan issues on. Everything partisan lives here.
* [What We Are, and Why We Cannot Be Taken Over](./what_we_are.mdx) — Member-owned, radically transparent, uncapturable, and the lesson the structure came from.

## Where this sits

This is one area of [We The Citizens R](/r/overview), the Republican front door at [WeCitizensR.com](https://wecitizensr.com). It is written for Republican voters, and nothing on the other three doors decides what belongs here.
