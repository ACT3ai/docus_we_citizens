# Our Board of Directors

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Who governs this?**
:::

This door has a board of directors. It sits at the .com level, with the community it governs. The non-partisan side does not have one.

* **Partisan governance belongs with the partisan community.** Decisions specific to the political right get made by a body answerable to the political right — not by a neutral site that takes no partisan position by design.
* **The .io keeps one plan, at the top level:** find the best candidates able to keep working for citizens. The many partisan plans live under this door and its board.
* **We will not overstate it.** It is a real governance body, and we describe it as exactly that and nothing more.
* **Its approvals are public.** The movement's approved data lives in a public repository, and that repository's history is the approval record — what was decided and when. There is no separate approvals database sitting behind it, and undoing a wrong call publishes too.
