# Charlie Kirk: Carrying the Work Forward

Charlie Kirk was assassinated, and the political right now needs a movement that doesn't depend on any one
leader, any one organization, or any one platform's permission. Three things have to be true for that
movement to survive. Citizens pick the voices on the stage, those voices can't be censored, and they can't be
cut off from the money that keeps them working.

## Three fixes, up front

1. **A conference that can't sell out.** At our [yearly conference](/r/yearly_conference), We The Citizens
   lets citizens vote on the political speakers. Nobody hands the stage to the donor class or to global elites.
   It stands in the slot AmFest holds today, and it is built to withstand the pressure to sell out.
2. **Influencers who can't be censored.** Their posts travel on open standards (Mastodon, Nostr and
   Bluesky). No single company holds the off switch, so one site blocking a post can't stop it from reaching
   everyone else.
3. **Influencers who can't be demonetized.** [WeCitizens.Social](https://m.wecitizens.social/) pays
   influencers 40% of the revenue from the people who sign up through them. No platform sits in the middle
   deciding whether they "qualify" this month.

These three are tied together. An influencer who gets paid but can be censored isn't free. One who can't be
censored but can't earn will eventually stop. And a movement full of free, paid, truth-telling voices still
needs citizens, not an organization, deciding who gets the microphone.

## A conference where citizens pick the speakers

A political movement is shaped by who stands on its stage. When a small group picks the speakers, that
small group is the thing an outside power has to win over, whether with money, access or pressure. Once it
is won over, every citizen in the audience is hearing voices someone else chose.

We The Citizens moves that decision to the citizens.

* **Citizens vote on the political speakers.** The speaker list comes out of
  [community voting](/r/community_voting) and [trust scores](/r/trust_scores) built on this door, in the
  open, not out of a back room.
* **The speaker list is the test.** Speakers who represent citizens, never people placed there by global
  elites or the donor class. See [how it replaces AmFest](/r/yearly_conference/replaces_amfest).
* **No money moves through it.** No fundraising, no donations, no campaign coordination. That is what makes
  "never sells out" something we can be held to, not just something we say.
* **It is open three ways.** [Open source, open data and open AI](/r/open_everything), with AI prompts
  that are plain to read, so anyone can check that the voting does what we say it does.

The conference is one part of a bigger job: [a replacement for TPUSA](/r/tpusa_replacement), where citizens
find the best political challengers to [replace incumbents](/r/replace_incumbents) who sell out and are
controlled by someone other than the voters.

## Influencers who can't be censored

Today a handful of companies own the social networks political speech runs on. Each one keeps its posts
locked inside its own walls, and each one can quietly turn off an influencer's audience with a ban, a
deboost or an isolated account. Whoever can lean on those few companies can decide which truths reach
citizens.

The fix is the same one the internet already uses. When one router drops traffic, the packets route around
it and still arrive. Social media posts need to work the same way.

* **Political social media has to move to open standards.** Mastodon, Nostr and Bluesky (the AT Protocol).
  Once a post lives on an open standard, no single company owns it.
* **[WeCitizens.Social](https://m.wecitizens.social/) is an open source X.com clone built on those
  standards.** Posts from Mastodon, Nostr and Bluesky already flow through it, and anyone can run a copy of
  it, the way anyone can run Linux.
* **Its feed ranking is transparent.** You can see why a post shows up where it does, so a quiet deboost has
  nowhere to hide.
* **One site can refuse to show a post. It can't stop the post from reaching everyone else.** That is the
  only way an influencer who exposes a puppet master stays heard.

This matters most for the question many citizens are still asking about Charlie Kirk's death: whether
something bigger was behind it. Investigators who ask that question can't be cut off from the public if their
posts travel on open standards. See how a citizen's work reaches that network in
[publishing to the social network](/features/publishing_to_the_social_network).

## A plan past Charlie Kirk

A movement built around one leader can be stopped by removing that leader. Political assassination is what
a captured system reaches for after money, blackmail and censorship have stopped working. The answer is a
movement with no single point of failure.

* **Power moves to the full US citizen base.** Not one leader who can be removed, but millions of citizens
  who can't be.
* **Political challengers who win in primaries.** Primaries are where a captured incumbent is most exposed,
  and where a citizen-chosen challenger can actually take the seat. See
  [replacing incumbents](/r/replace_incumbents).
* **Fix the laws.** Charlie Kirk disclosure laws, Epstein file disclosure laws and more, so the truth comes
  out through the record. See [fix bills](/r/fix_bills).
* **Expose who is pulling the strings.** See [foreign intelligence](/r/foreign_intelligence) and
  [exposing the deep state](/r/expose_deep_state).
