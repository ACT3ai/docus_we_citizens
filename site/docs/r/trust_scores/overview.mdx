# Trust Scores

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Where do I cast a vote that counts on a candidate?**
:::

Here. Trust scores are **built and voted at the party level**, and this is one of the party doors.

* **What they measure:** whether a candidate really works for citizens, and how hard they are to co-opt. The scoring reflects this community's judgment of independence and merit.
* **Where your vote counts:** on this door. Cast it here and it counts.
* **The non-partisan .io shows a combined view** stitched together from the party doors, and it is read-only. Nobody votes there.
* **They measure candidates, never citizens.** See [No Social Credit](/docs/r/no_social_credit).

## In this area

* [Built and Voted Here, at the Party Level](./built_and_voted_here.mdx) — If you want your vote to count on a score, this is where you cast it.
* [The Read-Only Roll-Up on the Non-Partisan Site](./the_read_only_rollup.mdx) — The .io stitches the party scores together. Nobody votes there.

## Where this sits

This is one area of [We The Citizens R](/docs/r/overview), the Republican front door at [WeCitizensR.com](https://wecitizensr.com). It is written for Republican voters, and nothing on the other three doors decides what belongs here.
