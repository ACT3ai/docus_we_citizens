# Replacing Incumbents Controlled by Global Elites

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Who exactly are you trying to replace, and how?**
:::

Get political challengers into power, and replace the incumbents controlled by global elites and the Deep State. That is the core action of this door.

* **Elections are the mechanism.** We beat captured incumbents at the ballot box — in the primary first, then in the general.
* **Incumbency earns nothing by itself.** No safe seats and no automatic re-endorsement. An incumbent who has stayed independent can be backed on the merits, like anybody else.
* **Party label is not the qualification. Independence from those forces is.** Plenty of captured incumbents have an R next to their name, and saying that out loud is what separates us from a party organ.

If you carry one sentence out of this door, carry that last one.

## How a seat actually changes hands

* **You are shown the seat, not the personality.** Each seat carries three people side by side: the incumbent, the best challenger from your own side, and the strongest challenger from any other party — same size photo, same arithmetic. People leave. The seat is the thing you vote on.
* **The record is traced end to end.** The problem, the enacted law that carries it, who voted it in, the bill that would undo it, and who kept that bill from ever reaching a vote.
* **Blocking a vote is counted as the heaviest act there is.** A member who votes against a repair took a position in public and can be answered for it at an election. A member who made sure the vote never happened erased the record itself — and erased every other member's chance to be counted.
* **A primary is the cheapest place to win.** You already agree with the party label. All that has to be shown is that this particular incumbent is not delivering on it.
