# How This Door Relates to the Other Three

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Is this a Republican-only movement?**
:::

No. There are four party doors on one app: **R** Republicans, **D** Democrats, **L** Libertarians, **S** Democratic Socialists. This is R.

* **Each door belongs to its own community.** This one is written for Republican voters and is free to differ from the other three in any way. There is no pairing and no mirror between them.
* **Every door respects the others.** We are replacing captured incumbents. We are not going after the voters behind another door.
* **One app, not four.** Same product, same principles, same arithmetic. WeCitizensR.com is edition R of the one We The Citizens app, recognized by the address you came in on.
