# No China-Style Social Credit in America

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Could a social credit system happen here?**
:::

A social credit system — a score that decides what a citizen is allowed to do — never gets built in America.

* **That includes the private-sector versions.** Debanking, de-platforming as punishment, and scoring citizens by political behavior all count. It does not have to be run by a government to do the same job to you.
* **Outlawed before it arrives, not after.** After is too late. The infrastructure is the hard part, and infrastructure does not get dismantled once it exists. Candidates get measured on that vote.
* **This is not what our own trust scores are.** Those measure candidates who are asking you for power. They never measure citizens. See [Trust Scores](/docs/r/trust_scores).

## Why our own system cannot turn into one

* **What you believe never reaches what you are allowed to do.** Your political placement is walled off from your account standing and from every award. That is a one-way rule in the software, not a promise we are asking you to take on faith.
* **The party question is not a gate.** No feature is denied on it, no page is hidden behind it, you can change it whenever you want, and changing it does not wipe anything you already did.
* **You never have to have a name.** Reading, reasoning, recording your positions and running the checks all work with no account at all, and a pseudonym can reach the top of the ladder. A system that punished anonymity would push exactly the citizens most at risk into either danger or irrelevance.
