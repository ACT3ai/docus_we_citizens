# Voting on Where You Sit on the Spectrum

Citizens vote their own position. That position drives candidate matching.

* Citizens vote their own position on the political spectrum, rather than being assigned one.
* That position drives [candidate matching](/docs/r/meritocracy) and shows the real distribution inside the community.
* It also keeps a loud minority from being mistaken for the whole — which is the failure mode of every un-measured community.
