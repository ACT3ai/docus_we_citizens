# No Needless or Forever Wars, and No Draft

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Where do you stand on war?**
:::

Wars that serve global elites and not citizens are exactly what this door opposes.

* **Forever wars are a symptom of captured incumbents.** That is why this sits on this door at all — it is a capture problem before it is a foreign-policy problem.
* **No draft.** Citizens are not raw material for someone else's foreign policy.
* **Candidates get measured on their actual war votes**, not their speeches — authorizations, appropriations, and the votes nobody expected to be read back to them.

This is the plank, not a full foreign-policy doctrine. It is deliberately narrow so it can be checked.

## How a war vote gets read

* **Two acts are chargeable on their own:** committing money or debt at a scale that forecloses future choice, and compelling a citizen's body or labour into state service.
* **The stage is the point.** A member who votes to table the amendment that would have stripped a provision, then votes no on final passage, has two records — and only both together are honest. Reporting the final vote alone is how a war record gets laundered.
* **"It was in the defense bill" is priced, not erased.** A floor vote inside a must-pass bundle counts for less than the same vote in the small room where the provision was written. It still counts.
