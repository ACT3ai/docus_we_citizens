# Removing Voter Fraud for Good

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Can I trust the count?**
:::

A citizen has to be able to believe the count. Without that, nothing else on this platform matters.

* The goal is not fraud reduced or fraud investigated. It is fraud made structurally impossible.
* We ask for verifiable elections, not for anyone's word.
* We do not make claims about a specific fraud we cannot source.

## In this area

* [Forever Eliminated, Not Merely Investigated](./forever_eliminated.mdx) — Every step from registration to certification, checkable by an ordinary citizen.
* [Auditable Technology, So Nobody Has to Trust a Vendor](./auditable_technology.mdx) — Open, auditable technology is the only kind an election can be built on.
