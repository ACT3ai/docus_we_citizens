# Forever Eliminated, Not Merely Investigated

Every step from registration to certification, checkable by an ordinary citizen.

* The goal is not reduced and it is not investigated. It is structurally impossible.
* Every step from registration to certification should be checkable by an ordinary citizen — not only by an authorized official.
* Candidates are measured on whether they will vote for that.
