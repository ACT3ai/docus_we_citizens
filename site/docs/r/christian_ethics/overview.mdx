# Great Ethics, Like Christian Ethics

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Where do your ethics come from?**
:::

Great ethics, and on this door we name them: Christian ethics. That is deliberate, and it is how this audience already talks.

* **Ethics first is the standard the whole movement runs on.** Whether a candidate can be moved by money or by pressure is an ethical question before it is a political one.
* **This door names where that standard comes from, in its own language.** The other three doors describe the same standard in theirs.
* **It is not a membership test.** Nobody is asked what they believe in order to use this. It is not a recruiting pitch and it is not a requirement.
* **It stays here.** No religion appears on the non-partisan .io, or on the Democrat, Libertarian or Democratic Socialist doors.
