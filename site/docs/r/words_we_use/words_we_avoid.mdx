# Words We Avoid

The phrases that never appear on this door.

* Anything that reads as the non-partisan voice. That copy lives on the .io and does not belong here.
* Attacks on the voters behind the D, L or S doors. Their captured incumbents are fair game; the voters are not.
* Any promised political outcome, any claimed endorsement, and any religion other than the Christian-values language above.
