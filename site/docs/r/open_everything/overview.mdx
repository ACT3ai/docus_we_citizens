# Open Source, Open Data, Open AI

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Why should I believe your numbers?**
:::

Nobody has to trust us, because nobody can check a black box. Every layer that decides something about a candidate or a vote is open to inspection.

* This is what makes the [meritocracy](/docs/r/meritocracy) claim verifiable instead of merely asserted.
* Three layers, all open: the code, the data, and the AI method.
* Where something is private by design — a citizen's own local files — we say so plainly instead of pretending everything is public.

## In this area

* [Open Source — The Code](./open_source_code.mdx) — The code that runs the voting, the matching and the scoring is open source.
* [Open Data](./open_data.mdx) — Citizens can re-run the numbers themselves and get the same answer.
* [Open AI](./open_ai.mdx) — Open models where we can, open prompts and open method always.
