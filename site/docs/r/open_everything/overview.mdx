# Open Source, Open Data, Open AI

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Why should I believe your numbers?**
:::

You should not have to believe them. You should be able to check them. Nobody can check a black box, so every layer that decides something about a candidate or a vote is open to inspection.

* **The code** that runs the voting, the matching and the scoring is open source. Read it, build it, confirm it does what we say it does.
* **The data** behind the trust scores, the candidate records and the community votes is open. Re-run the numbers yourself and get the same answer.
* **The AI method** is open: open models where we can, open prompts and open method always. You can see why it concluded what it concluded, and disagree with it. It advises citizens. It never decides for them.
* **What is private stays private.** A citizen's own local files are private by design, and we say that plainly instead of pretending everything is public.

This is what makes the [meritocracy](/docs/r/meritocracy) claim verifiable instead of merely asserted.
