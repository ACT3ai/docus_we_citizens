# An Ethical, Clean Economy That Is Ideal for Workers

Removing the rigged layer is the means. The clean economy is the end.

* **The healthy economy is not the target.** People building, hiring and taking real risk are the part that works. Keep that; take the layer off the top of it.
* The destination is an economy that is ethical and clean — not merely one where a different set of people is holding the levers.
* It has to be ideal for workers too. On this door that is said plainly, and it is not a concession to anybody.
* Removing the rigged layer is the means. The clean economy is the end. Do not confuse the two.
