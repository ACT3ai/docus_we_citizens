# US Intelligence Answers to US Citizens

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Can an agency decide my election?**
:::

Not if it answers to citizens. US intelligence services answer to US citizens, or they are not ours.

* **The failure mode** is a service taking direction from non-citizens, from foreign money, or from a private power that never stood for election.
* **Leverage over officeholders is how capture actually works.** Collected files, selective leaks, and briefings used as pressure all count.
* **Oversight you can verify**, not oversight you are told exists.
* **Candidates get measured on whether they will impose that control**, not on whether they say they support it.

Foreign services are the [next area](/docs/r/foreign_intelligence). This one is about our own.
