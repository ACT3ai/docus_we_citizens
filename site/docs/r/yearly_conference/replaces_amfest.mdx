# Replaces AmFest

The speaker list is the test.

* This is the right's strategy conference, standing in the slot AmFest holds today.
* The speaker list is the test: people who represent citizens, never people placed there by global elites or by the donor class.
* A conference the donor class can buy a slot at is a conference the donor class owns.
* At AmFest the organizers choose who is on stage. Here citizens vote on every speaker, 1 to 5 thumbs up or 1 to 5 thumbs down, and the tallies are public.
* Do not describe it as a movement-wide non-partisan event.
