# Yearly Conference on Political Right Strategy

Once a year this community meets and sets its own direction for the year ahead. It stands in the slot AmFest holds today.

* **The speaker list is the test.** People who represent citizens — never people put on a stage by global elites or by the donor class.
* **The direction gets set there, together.** It does not get announced there.
* **It is this door's event.** Each party door runs its own, at the .com level. It is not a movement-wide non-partisan event, and we do not describe it as one.
* **No money moves through it.** No fundraising, no donations, no campaign coordination. That is what makes "speakers who represent citizens" a thing we can be held to rather than a thing we say.
* **Citizens vote on the speakers.** Every person on the list goes to this community by name: 1 to 5 thumbs up, or 1 to 5 thumbs down. One vote per citizen per person, change it whenever you like, and the tallies are public. At AmFest the organizers pick who is on stage. Here you say who belongs there.
* **Invited is not the same as confirmed.** Everyone on the 2027 poster has been invited, and anyone who has not confirmed carries a small star until they do.
