## Why These People

Every person on this list stood with Charlie Kirk — not people who stood to profit from his absence. They are thought leaders, elected officials, journalists, and advocates who have demonstrated commitment to the principles Charlie fought for: ethics first, citizens first, truth first.

## Board Commitments

Once accepted, every board member is bound by the charter:

* **Term-limited positions** with member recall authority
* **No conflicts of interest** with corporate sponsors or foreign entities
* **Full financial disclosure** — required to disclose all financial interests publicly
* **Charter-bound** — serve member interests above all else
* **Elected by members** on regular cycles with annual independent audits

## Board Governance

The board makes policy decisions and carries out the charter. The board does not own the organization — the members do. Every major decision flows from the membership. Any board member can be recalled by member vote at any time.

All board votes are public. All board decisions are documented and published on this website. This is not optional — it is a requirement of the charter.

For full governance details, see the [Charter](/docs/intro#governance).
