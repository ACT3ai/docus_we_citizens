# The forever war pattern

A position that every party base holds and no majority ever delivers is a **capture signal**, not a
coincidence. This is the clearest case on the site, because the voting has already been done — in
both directions, repeatedly — and the outcome did not follow.

## The four mechanics

### 1. The authorisation outlives what it authorised

An authorisation passed for one thing gets used for another, in another country, a decade later,
against people who were not born when it passed. Nobody is required to renew it, so nobody has to
defend it, so it never gets voted on again. **Renewal is the whole fight**, and the absence of a
renewal requirement is the arrangement.

### 2. The cost lands away from the say

The people who bear it — in service, in casualties, in the districts that supply both — are
systematically not the people who decide. That decoupling is not incidental; it is what allows a
policy with no constituency to survive indefinitely.

### 3. Ending it is made expensive to whoever ends it

Every withdrawal is a political event with a named owner, and every continuation is nothing at all.
That asymmetry is enough on its own to explain the outcome without anybody intending it — which is
why the fix has to be structural rather than a matter of electing braver people.

### 4. Each administration inherits the arrangement, not the choice

The new arrival is handed a running commitment, a set of standing obligations and a briefing about
what leaving would cost. The choice was made before they arrived, by people who will still be there
after they leave. That is the same durability signature described in
**[A globalist deep state](../congress_for_citizens/the_globalist_deep_state.mdx)**.

## The gap between the vote and the outcome is the finding

Voters have ended these wars at the ballot box, in both parties, more than once. They did not end.
That gap — what was voted for, versus what happened — is the documented item, and it is filed like
every other one in **[Problems](../problems/overview.mdx)**.

It is worth being precise about what this establishes: not that any particular official lied, but
that **the decision is not being made where citizens were told it is made.** That is a claim about a
mechanism, it is checkable from the public record, and it does not require assuming bad faith by
anybody.
