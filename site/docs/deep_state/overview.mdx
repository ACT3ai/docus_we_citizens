# Exposing the Deep State

<CTA />

This is the area with the strictest rules on the site, because it is the area where being wrong costs
the most. Nothing here is asserted. Everything is sourced, or it is marked as missing and the
withholding is recorded as its own fact.

## What we mean by the terms

Used loosely, these words mean nothing. Used here, each has a definition that can fail:

* **Deep state** — permanent, unelected decision-making power that persists across administrations
  and is not answerable to any electorate.
* **[Global elites](/congress_for_citizens/the_globalist_deep_state/)** — interests that operate above and across national constituencies, whose
  positions survive elections in any one country.
* **The Epstein class** — the network of people implicated in the Epstein matter by the public
  record, including sealed and court-produced material, as released.

A person belongs in one of these categories on this site only when the **public record** puts them
there. Where the record is an allegation, it is published as an allegation, attributed to its source.

## The documented crimes, and the record behind each

Each item is a file, not a claim: what is alleged, who alleges it, which document or proceeding it
came from, and what is still missing. They live alongside the rest of the catalog in
**[Problems](../problems/overview.mdx)** and can be cloned and checked from
**[Open Data](../open_data/overview.mdx)**.

## The withheld-files pattern

Three long-running examples, and the same shape in all three:

* **The Epstein files** — material withheld from the public.
* **The JFK assassination records** — records withheld decades past every stated justification.
* **The Charlie Kirk files** — material withheld from the public.

What the pattern establishes is not the content of the missing pages. It is that **the party being
asked about controls what the public is allowed to see** — and that the withholding is
decades-durable, survives changes of administration, and carries no penalty for anyone.

That is a finding on its own. A gap that only ever runs in one direction is evidence about who
controls the gap. See **[Evidence](../evidence/overview.mdx)** for how a chain of links is reasoned
about when some of the links are missing on purpose, and
**[Problem Laws](../problem_laws/overview.mdx)** for the statutes that make withholding costless.

## The sourcing rule

**No claim about a living person is published without a source.** Where a matter has not been proven
in court, it is described as alleged and attributed. This is not softness — an unsourced claim is the
easiest thing in the world to discredit, and discrediting one is how a whole record gets dismissed.
