# The documented crimes

This is the strictest area on the site, because it is the one where being wrong costs the most —
costs the people wrongly named, and costs every other true thing on this site by association.

So nothing here is a claim. **Everything here is a file.**

## What a file contains

Every entry carries the same five parts, and it does not get published without all five:

1. **What is alleged**, stated narrowly enough to be wrong.
2. **Who alleges it** — the plaintiff, the prosecutor, the committee, the inspector general, the
   named witness. Never "reports suggest".
3. **Which document or proceeding it came from**, linked to the primary source. An indictment, a
   deposition, a released record, a court finding.
4. **What its status is** — alleged, charged, established by a court, dismissed, unresolved. These
   are different words on purpose and the file never blurs them.
5. **What is still missing**, named as an open gap rather than filled with inference.

## Alleged, charged, and established are three different things

The difference is not a technicality; it is most of the information:

* **Alleged** — somebody with standing said it, on a record. The fact is that they said it.
* **Charged** — a prosecutor filed it. Still not established.
* **Established by a court** — a finder of fact concluded it. Only here does the site state it as
  fact about a living person.

A file that moves between these states is updated, and the change is published as a correction rather
than silently applied.

## Where the files live

Alongside the rest of the catalog in **[Problems](../problems/overview.mdx)**, cloneable in full from
**[Open Data](../open_data/overview.mdx)**. You do not need our software or our website to check any
of it — that is the point of publishing it as a repository rather than a database.

## How a chain is reasoned about when links are missing

Most of these records have holes in them, some of them created deliberately. The method for
reasoning across a chain with missing links — and for saying honestly how much the conclusion is
worth — is **[Chain of evidence](../evidence/chain_of_evidence.mdx)** and
**[Your confidence threshold](../evidence/your_confidence_threshold.mdx)**.
