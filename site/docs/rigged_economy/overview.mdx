# The Rigged Economy

<CTA />

"Rigged" is usually said as a feeling. Here it has to be said as a **finding** — a specific rule, a
specific beneficiary, and the record that shows the two are connected.

## What "rigged" means concretely

Not that some people do better than others. That some people **write the rule they compete under**.
The concrete forms are:

* **Rules bought rather than argued** — a provision that appears in a bill with no constituency
  behind it except the party it benefits.
* **Costs socialised, gains kept** — losses land on the public balance sheet; the upside does not.
* **Barriers dressed as standards** — a requirement that a large incumbent can absorb and a new
  entrant cannot, sold as safety.
* **Access as the real currency** — the decisive input is who you can reach, not what you can build.

## Documenting it yourself, rather than being told

The point of this area is that a citizen base can **out and identify** the rigging on its own, with
the same tools we use:

1. **Name one rule.** One provision, one rate, one exemption — not "the system".
2. **Find who it is for.** Who has to comply, and who is carved out.
3. **Follow the record.** Filings, [roll calls](/docs/voting_records/overview/), contracts, disclosures — [sourced at the door](/docs/open_data/the_sourcing_rule/).
4. **Record the gaps.** Where the record has been withheld, that withholding is itself the finding.
   See **[Evidence](../evidence/overview.mdx)**.
5. **File it as a problem.** It joins the ranked, sourced catalog in
   **[Problems](../problems/overview.mdx)** and can be argued with in public.

## The fix has to be one both sides will sign

A fix that only one party's base supports is not a fix — it is a plank, and it lasts exactly as long
as that party's majority. Because the rigging [damages both bases](/docs/non_partisan_site/what_crosses_both_parties/), the durable version is written to
be **signable by both**, which means:

* It targets the **mechanism**, not the industry — the carve-out and the disclosure gap, not a list
  of villains.
* It survives a change of control, because both bases already agreed to it.
* It comes out of **[Fixing the Law](../fix_laws/overview.mdx)** as actual bill text, not a slogan.

## An economy that is ethical and works for workers

The end state both bases describe, in their own vocabulary, is the same one: an economy where the
rules are the same for everyone, where work is worth doing, and where nobody's advantage came from
having written the rule. That is the standard this area is measured against — see
**[Great American Ethics](../american_ethics/overview.mdx)**.

## What is not in this area

Party-specific economic packages. Each party base builds its own on its own `.com` door — see
**[One App, Four Doors](../four_doors/overview.mdx)**.
