# Document it yourself

The point of this area is that the citizen base can **identify the rigging on its own**, without
waiting for an institution to confirm it. Nothing here is reserved for us. These are the same five
steps our own records go through.

## The five steps

### 1. Name one rule

One provision, one rate, one exemption, one contract. Not "the system", not "the banks", not "both
parties". A finding that cannot be pointed at cannot be checked, and a claim that cannot be checked
gets dismissed by the first person with an incentive to dismiss it.

### 2. Find who it is for

Who has to comply, and who is carved out. The carve-out is usually the whole story — a rule with an
exemption written into it is a rule about who the exemption is for.

### 3. Follow the record

Filings, roll calls, contracts, disclosures, court records. **The primary document, linked at the
door** — not an article about the document. If a claim's only source is somebody else's claim, it is
not sourced yet.

### 4. Record the gaps

Where the record has been withheld, that withholding is written down as its own fact. A gap somebody
created on purpose is a finding about who controls the gap, and it is more useful than a guess about
what was in it. See **[Naming the gaps](../evidence/naming_the_gaps.mdx)** and
**[Chain of evidence](../evidence/chain_of_evidence.mdx)**.

### 5. File it as a problem

It joins the ranked, sourced catalog in **[Problems](../problems/overview.mdx)**, where it can be
argued with in public, ranked against everything else, and picked up by somebody who wants to work on
it. **[Report a problem law](../problem_laws/report_a_problem_law.mdx)** is the route when the
finding is a statute.

## What the AI does, and what it never does

The assistant reads long material against published rubrics, checks reasoning for gaps, and finds the
document you asked for. It **never decides what is published about a person**, never fills a gap with
a plausible guess, and never sources a claim to itself. See **[AI](../ai/overview.mdx)** and
**[What the AI never decides](../ai/what_the_ai_never_decides.mdx)**.

## Rules about people, in this area specifically

Economic findings tend to have names attached. The restraint is the same as everywhere else on this
site: **arithmetic over the public record is fair game; an accusation of a crime is not, unless a
court established it.** Everything short of that is attributed to its source, in the source's own
framing.
