# The cross-party forum

The second job of this door. Not a place to argue across party lines — there is no shortage of those
and they change nothing. A place to **decide**.

## Decision venue, not debate venue

The difference is procedural, and it is the whole design:

* **Positions are recorded, pooled and counted.** You state where you stand, with your reasoning and
  the evidence you actually have. See **[Decisions](../decisions/overview.mdx)**.
* **Nobody is averaged away.** The platform always shows you the delta between your position and
  where the community landed, rather than quietly folding you into a majority.
* **The output is an artefact**, not a thread: a named problem, a sourced record, and a fix.
* **The unit of judgement is a person and a vote**, never a party. No page here characterises a
  party, compares parties, or attributes motive to one.

## The bar the output has to clear

> **A Republican and a Democrat can each sign it without pretending to agree about everything else.**

That is a demanding standard and it is why the forum works on mechanism rather than on outcomes. A
disclosure requirement, a sunset clause, a repealed carve-out — these can be agreed by people who
want opposite things from the economy, because they are about who gets to see and who gets to
decide, not about what the answer should be.

A fix only one base supports is not a fix. It is a plank, and it lasts exactly as long as that
party's majority.

## Who is in the room

Real, verified citizens — which is a technical requirement, not a slogan. A forum anyone can
manufacture people into is decided by whoever has the largest budget for accounts. [The mesh](/trust_scores/the_mesh_and_what_it_defends_against/) of
verified citizens is what makes a purchased crowd worthless; see
**[Trust Scores](../trust_scores/overview.mdx)**. Anonymity is preserved throughout: a pseudonymous
citizen can reach the top of [the trust ladder](/meritocracy/the_trust_ladder/), because the question is whether one real person is
behind an account, never who they are.

## What comes out of it

* A **[problem record](/problems/what_is_a_problem_record/)**, ranked and sourced — **[Problems](../problems/overview.mdx)**.
* A **fix bill**, in actual legislative text — **[Fix Bills](../good_bills/overview.mdx)**.
* An **action** a citizen can take this week — **[Take Action](../take_action/overview.mdx)**.
