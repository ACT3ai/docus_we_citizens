# What it is

"We The Citizens" names three things, and it is worth separating them early because people arrive
looking for different ones.

## 1. The movement

A political movement whose claim is narrow and testable: **representative democracy is not working the
way it was promised, this is measurable against the social contract, and citizens can do something
about it without waiting for permission.**

It is not a party. It does not run candidates as its own. It has no position on most policy questions,
deliberately — the yardstick it uses is about the *mechanism* of self-government, not about outcomes.

## 2. The app

Software a citizen runs — usually **on their own computer**, sometimes hosted — that does the actual
work: record positions, build evidence chains, read the public record, assess candidates against
published rubrics, log what you did about it.

It is local-first. Your positions, your value systems and your evidence chains are flat files on your
own disk. No server is required, and nothing leaves your machine unless you choose to publish it.

## 3. The public record

A git repository containing everything the movement asserts: the problems, the laws, the bills, the
politicians, the rubrics, the provenance of every download. You can clone it and check any claim
without running our software or trusting our website.

## How they fit together

```
the RECORD     public, cloneable, sourced        <- the evidence
the APP        reads the record on your machine  <- the reasoning
the MOVEMENT   what citizens do with the answer  <- the action
```

Each layer is useful without the ones above it. You can read the record and never run the app. You can
run the app and never join anything.

## What it is not

**Not a news site.** It does not tell you what happened this week.

**Not a social network.** There is a paired one, and it is deliberately a separate property.

**Not an oracle.** The AI in it checks reasoning and reads long-form material against published
rubrics. It never decides what is true, and it never publishes anything on its own.
