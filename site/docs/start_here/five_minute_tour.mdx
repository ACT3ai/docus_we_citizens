# Five minute tour

Read in this order and the rest of the site will make sense.

## Why anything needs fixing

**[The Problem](../the_problem/overview.mdx)** — the deadlock pattern: classify a slice of the record,
block anyone from recovering it, then demand 100% proof before anybody is allowed to act.

**[The Social Contract](../social_contract/overview.mdx)** — what we measure against, so that
"misalignment" is a claim with a yardstick rather than a mood.

## What the movement is doing about it

**[Problems](../problems/overview.mdx)** — the ranked catalog of what is wrong, each entry sourced and
broken into separable root causes.

**[Laws](../laws/overview.mdx)** — and its four libraries: the
[laws doing the damage](../problem_laws/overview.mdx), the
[process of drafting repairs](../fix_laws/overview.mdx), the
[bills we wrote](../good_bills/overview.mdx), and the
[repairs somebody actually filed](../good_laws/overview.mdx).

## The people

**[Politicians](../politicians/overview.mdx)** — the doctrine first: everything published about a
politician is an award earned or not earned, and there is a wall between the voting record and
everything else.

Then the rosters — [elected](../legacy_politicians/overview.mdx) and
[new leaders](../new_politicians/overview.mdx) — and the three awards:
[Monkey](../monkey/overview.mdx), [Llama](../llama/overview.mdx),
[Flamingo](../flamingo/overview.mdx).

## The four pillars

[Decisions](../decisions/overview.mdx) · [Fixing the law](../fix_laws/overview.mdx) ·
[Values](../values/overview.mdx) · [Evidence](../evidence/overview.mdx)

Pillar 4 — Evidence — is the intellectual core. Everything else is downstream of being allowed to reach
a conclusion without the last five percent.

## Doing something

**[Karma](../karma/overview.mdx)** and **[Meritocracy](../meritocracy/overview.mdx)** — how the work is
recorded, and how it earns a citizen more weight without ever earning them authority.
