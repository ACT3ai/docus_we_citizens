# Community-Based Voting and Collaboration

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Who decides what this community stands for?**
:::

Community-based voting and collaboration is the mechanism the whole platform runs on. Positions, plans and candidate judgments come from citizens voting and working together.

* On this door, it is democratic socialists doing that work for their own community.
* Nothing here is a platform delivered downward to members. The community produces it.
* Our job is to run the process honestly and keep it hard to capture.

## In this area

* [The Community Builds the Package, We Do Not Hand It Down](./the_community_builds_it.mdx) — Collaboration produces the package, and the package is the product.
* [Voting on Where You Sit on the Spectrum](./vote_your_spectrum.mdx) — Citizens vote their own position. That position drives candidate matching.
