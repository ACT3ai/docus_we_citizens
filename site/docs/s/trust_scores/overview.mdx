# Trust Scores

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Where do I cast a vote that counts on a candidate?**
:::

Trust scores are how a citizen judges whether a candidate really works for citizens. They are **built at the per-party level**, which is the .com doors, and this is one of them.

* Voting on them happens here. Not on the .io.
* Scoring reflects this community's judgment of independence and merit.
* The .io shows a combined view stitched from the party doors, and it is read-only.

## In this area

* [Built and Voted Here, at the Party Level](./built_and_voted_here.mdx) — If you want your vote to count on a score, this is where you cast it.
* [The Read-Only Roll-Up on the Non-Partisan Site](./the_read_only_rollup.mdx) — The .io stitches the party scores together. Nobody votes there.

## Where this sits

This is one area of [We The Citizens S](/docs/s/overview), the Democratic Socialist front door at [WeCitizensSocialism.com](https://wecitizenssocialism.com). Its mirror on the other side of the axis is [We The Citizens L](/docs/l/overview) — same area, same shape, party label swapped.
