# Meritocracy on This Community's Own Basis

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**How do you decide who is 'best' without just picking favorites?**
:::

Meritocracy is the engine underneath everything on this platform: find the candidates most dedicated to always work for citizens, and strongest at pushing off being co-opted.

* Shared with the .io and with all four doors. The engine does not change when the door does.
* What this door adds is the best meritocracy **basis** for this community to find its own best people — the community decides what merit means here, not a neutral baseline and not us.
* The arithmetic that turns this into a number lives in [Trust Scores](/docs/s/trust_scores).

## In this area

* [Dedicated to Always Work for Citizens](./dedicated_to_citizens.mdx) — The test is what happens after the election, not what was promised before it.
* [Strong Enough to Refuse Being Co-Opted](./strong_enough_to_refuse.mdx) — Being good is not enough if the pressure turns you.
* [Matching Candidates to Where You Sit on the Spectrum](./matching_the_spectrum.mdx) — Democratic socialists are not one position, so we do not flatten them into one.
* [Open Data, Open Source, Open AI](./open_source_open_data_open_ai.mdx) — This is what makes the meritocracy claim verifiable instead of just asserted.
