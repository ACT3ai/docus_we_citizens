# Meritocracy on This Community's Own Basis

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**How do you decide who is 'best' without just picking favorites?**
:::

By track record, not by who we like — and on a basis **this community sets**, not one we hand down. Meritocracy is the engine underneath everything here: find the candidates most dedicated to always work for citizens, and strongest at pushing off being co-opted.

The same engine runs on all four doors. What this door adds is the **basis** for judging merit: democratic socialists decide what counts as merit here, not a neutral baseline and not us. The arithmetic that turns it into a number is in [Trust Scores](/docs/s/trust_scores).

## What "best" is measured from

* **Measured after the election, not promised before it.** What a candidate says on the trail is not evidence. What they did with a vote is.
* **Two separate records, never blended into one.** What someone did with power — how they voted, what they authored, what they blocked — is judged on its own. What they said, in interviews and public commitments, is judged separately. A good speech never moves a voting-record result.
* **Most challengers have no votes to read**, so their own recorded words are the whole evidence base. A newcomer is not penalized for never having held the seat.
* **You do not have to take our word for the method.** The code, the data and the AI method are all open, and the AI reads and reasons but never awards anything and never approves anything. See [Open Data, Open Source, Open AI](./open_source_open_data_open_ai.mdx).
* **How much we know is published next to the result.** "We know 31% about this person" sits beside it. Somebody can be fully known and still not good.
