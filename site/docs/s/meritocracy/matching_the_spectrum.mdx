# Matching Candidates to Where You Sit on the Spectrum

Democratic socialists are not one position, so we do not flatten them into one.

* Democratic socialists are not one position. Treating them as one is how a community gets captured by whoever is loudest.
* Voters vote on where they sit on the political spectrum, and candidates get matched to that.
* The match is a fit **to the voter**, not a ranking handed down by us.
