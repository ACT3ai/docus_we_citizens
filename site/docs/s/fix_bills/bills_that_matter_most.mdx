# Bills That Matter Most to Democratic Socialists

A concrete bill is a better test of a candidate than a general statement.

* There will be specific standing bills this community cares about more than others.
* Those get named and tracked, so a candidate can be measured against them.
* A concrete bill is a better test of a candidate than a general statement. Ask about the bill.
