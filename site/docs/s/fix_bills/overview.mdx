# Partisan Laws and Bills

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Do you have actual legislation?**
:::

The .com doors are where partisan law work happens. The .io does not host it. This is where democratic socialists add and prioritize the laws they care about.

* Legislation here is generated from real problems citizens raise, not from a wish list.
* Prioritized by the community, by the same voting and collaboration as everything else.
* Partisan by design. That is the point of a party door.

## In this area

* [Bills That Matter Most to Democratic Socialists](./bills_that_matter_most.mdx) — A concrete bill is a better test of a candidate than a general statement.
