# Who This Door Is For

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Is this site for me?**
:::

If you are a democratic socialist, yes. This is the room where this community does its own work — finding challengers, naming the laws it cares about, and judging the people who ask for its votes.

* **Who it is for.** Democratic socialists.
* **Who it is not for.** A visitor who wants the neutral version goes to [WeTheCitizens.io](/) instead. The .io is the lobby. This is the room where the work happens.
* **The promise.** Your side builds its own package here, in your own language. Nobody hands it down to you.
* **What we are not.** No fundraising, no donations, no campaign coordination, and no endorsement of a party. We back people, and we publish why.

**We are not here to write your program.** What this community wants is this community's business — you vote it, you decide it, you pick who carries it. What we fix is the part that keeps eating it: representatives who get captured by powerful interests and stop working for the people who elected them. Win a seat with somebody who cannot be bought, and the program is yours to pass.

* [Democratic Socialists, Not the Establishment](./democratic_socialists_not_the_establishment.mdx) — We are talking to the voter, not to the officeholder and not to the party apparatus.

## Where this sits

This is one area of [We The Citizens S](/docs/s/overview), the Democratic Socialist front door at [WeCitizensSocialism.com](https://wecitizenssocialism.com). It is written for democratic socialists, and nothing on the other three doors decides what belongs here.
