# Who This Door Is For

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Is this site for me?**
:::

This door is for democratic socialists. It is not a general-audience site, and it is not trying to be one.

* **Who it is for.** Democratic socialists.
* **Who it is not for.** A visitor who wants the neutral version goes to [WeTheCitizens.io](/) instead. The .io is the lobby; this is the room where the work happens.
* **The promise.** This is where your side builds its own package, in your own language.

## In this area

* [Democratic Socialists, Not the Establishment](./democratic_socialists_not_the_establishment.mdx) — We are talking to the voter, not to the officeholder and not to the party apparatus.
* [Why You Are Here and Not on the Non-Partisan Site](./why_here_not_the_io.mdx) — The .io is deliberately neutral and will not take your partisan issues on. Everything partisan lives here.

## Where this sits

This is one area of [We The Citizens S](/docs/s/overview), the Democratic Socialist front door at [WeCitizensSocialism.com](https://wecitizenssocialism.com). This door is written for Democratic Socialist voters from marketing/messaging/socialism.md, and its pages answer to that file alone — no other edition constrains what belongs here.
