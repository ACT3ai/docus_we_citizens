# Challengers Who Cannot Be Bought

An incumbent serving foreign interests over the citizen base is disqualified by that fact.

* The threat is any powerful force that is not the citizen: concentrated money, corporate and donor power, foreign governments, and intelligence services.
* An incumbent serving foreign interests over the citizen base is disqualified by that fact, whatever else is on their record.
* We look at whether a group is running narrative control over who gets elected — and we name the category, not individual people we cannot substantiate.
