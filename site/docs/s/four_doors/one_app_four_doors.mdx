# One App, Four Doors

WeCitizensSocialism.com is edition S of the one We The Citizens app, recognized by hostname.

* [WeCitizensSocialism.com](https://wecitizenssocialism.com) is edition **S** of the one We The Citizens app, recognized by hostname.
* Docs for this audience: [WeTheCitizens.io/s/](/s/overview).
* Not a separate product, a separate movement, or a separate set of principles. One app, rendered for the audience that arrived.
* The other three doors are [We The Citizens R](/r/overview), [We The Citizens D](/d/overview), [We The Citizens L](/l/overview). Each has its own audience, its own priorities and its own pages. None of them is this door's mirror, and a change here creates no obligation to any of them.
