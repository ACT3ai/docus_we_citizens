# The Yearly Strategic Conference

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Do you hold events?**
:::

Once a year this community meets and sets its own direction for the year ahead.

* **The direction gets set there, together.** It does not get announced there.
* **The speaker list is the test:** people who represent citizens, never people put on a stage by the donor class.
* **No money moves through it.** No fundraising, no donations, no campaign coordination. That is what makes the speaker-list test something we can be held to rather than something we say.
* **It is this door's event.** Each party door runs its own, at the .com level. It is not a movement-wide non-partisan event, and we do not describe it as one.

## In this area

* [A .com-Level Event, Not a Non-Partisan One](./a_com_level_event.mdx) — The conference is not held at the non-partisan level.

## Where this sits

This is one area of [We The Citizens S](/docs/s/overview), the Democratic Socialist front door at [WeCitizensSocialism.com](https://wecitizenssocialism.com). It is written for democratic socialists, and nothing on the other three doors decides what belongs here.
