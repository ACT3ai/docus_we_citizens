# An Economy Ideal for Workers in the Era of AI

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**What happens to my job when AI arrives?**
:::

Nobody knows yet, and anyone who tells you they do is selling something. What this community can do is settle the question while the answer is still being written, instead of arguing about it afterward.

* **The question this door asks:** who captures the gains from automation — the citizen base, or the same concentrated money that rigged the last economy.
* **The standard:** an economy that is ideal for workers in the era of AI is one where productivity gains reach the people doing the work.
* **The discipline:** we name the problem honestly rather than promising an outcome we cannot deliver.
* **Where it gets decided:** in law, written by whoever is holding the seats when it gets written. That is why this sits on a door about who holds them.
* This is the same fight as [Removing the Rigged Economy](/docs/s/rigged_economy), arriving one technology cycle early.
