# The Movement

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Who is behind this and what am I joining?**
:::

**We The Citizens** is a political movement and the company that builds its tools. One name covers
both, deliberately — there is no separate commercial entity with different interests.

## The charter, in one paragraph

A citizen-powered decision-making platform that fixes society's problems through collective
intelligence and AI-applied ethics. It runs in two modes: **Individual**, where one person runs it on
their own computer and defines their own positions, and **Community**, where many people's decisions
are pooled into a consensus. Individual Mode is the default and is complete on its own — **a citizen
never has to trust a central server to use the product.**

## The four pillars

1. **[Decisions](../decisions/overview.mdx)** — voting and policy positions, individually and pooled.
2. **[Law generation](../fix_laws/overview.mdx)** — turning named problems into legislative language.
3. **[Values](../values/overview.mdx)** — ethics captured in a form an AI can apply consistently.
4. **[Evidence](../evidence/overview.mdx)** — deciding under deliberately incomplete information.

Pillar 4 is the one the others depend on. Without it, every conclusion waits forever on a link somebody
is withholding on purpose.

## What joining means

Less than you might expect, and that is the point.

* **You do not have to join to use it.** On your own machine you can be an anonymous default user
  indefinitely.
* **You do not have to give up anonymity to matter.** A fully pseudonymous citizen can reach the top
  trust rung and full merit weight.
* **Money buys nothing.** A paid supporter gets **zero** merit weight for paying. That is written down
  as a rule so nobody can later present it as an obvious optimisation.
* **Nothing is auto-approved, ever.** Standing buys attention in a queue. Approval is always a human
  admin decision.
