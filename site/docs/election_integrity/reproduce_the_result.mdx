# Reproduce the result

The test this area is built around, stated so that it can fail:

> **An outside team, using only the public record, reproduces the official result.**

Read the words narrowly, because each of them is doing work.

## What each word rules out

* **Outside** — not the administering authority, and not anybody the administering authority chose.
* **Team** — plural, independent, and ideally including people who wanted the other outcome.
* **Only the public record** — no special access, no permission, no briefing, no login somebody has
  to grant.
* **Reproduces** — arrives at the same numbers by doing the work, not by being shown the work.
* **The official result** — the actual certified figures, not a sample or a summary.

Observing the process is not this test. Being briefed is not this test. Having a bipartisan panel
sign off is not this test.

## Failing it is a real finding, and not the finding people expect

An election that cannot be reproduced has **not** been shown to be wrong. It has been shown to be
**unverifiable**, which is a different, separate and serious thing.

Keeping those two apart is the entire integrity of this area. Conflating them — treating
*unverifiable* as *stolen* — is how a legitimate demand for checkable elections gets turned into a
claim that can be dismissed, and it has happened to this argument repeatedly in both directions.

## What this movement will never do

**Certify who won any particular election.** Not now, not later, on any door.

The moment a citizen platform starts declaring winners it becomes one more party asking to be
trusted, and it forfeits the only thing that made it useful. We publish the method, the data and the
gaps. **The conclusion is the citizen's.**

## What a citizen can check without permission

With public data and open code, a person can compare published totals against published detail in
their own jurisdiction, follow the arithmetic, and see exactly where the record runs out. That last
part is usually the most informative — see **[Audits and open gaps](./audits_and_open_gaps.mdx)** and
**[Naming the gaps](../evidence/naming_the_gaps.mdx)**.
