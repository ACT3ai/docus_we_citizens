# Challenger Profiles

Individual deep-dive profiles on people who meet the [Bonhoeffer Criteria](/docs/bonhoeffer-criteria) — or who are close enough to warrant serious attention. These profiles go beyond the summary entries on the [Politician Challengers](/docs/politician-challengers) and [Dietrich Bonhoeffers](/docs/dietrich-bonhoeffers) rosters and document each person's background, public record, and scored criteria in detail.

## Who Gets a Profile

A profile is warranted when:

* The person has a substantial public record — enough to score all 15 criteria with real evidence
* Their background and evolution are relevant to understanding their current stance
* The person is actively influential and likely to remain so

Not every person on the roster has a profile yet. Profiles are added as research is completed.
