# AI

The movement uses AI heavily, and it is worth being blunt about the shape of that, because "an AI
scores politicians" is a fair thing to be suspicious of.

**The AI is a verifier, not an oracle.** Its job is to check whether reasoning holds, to read enormous
amounts of material consistently, and to apply rules that were written down in public before it ran.
It does not decide what is true, and it does not decide what you should think.

## What it actually does

* **Checks a chain of reasoning from first principles.** Not *an authority said so* — *does this
  actually follow?* This is the machinery behind Pillar 4: a citizen with 95% of an evidence chain can
  have the logic checked rather than being told to wait for the missing 5% that was withheld on
  purpose.
* **Reads long-form material against a published rubric.** Hundreds of hours of interviews, assessed
  against criteria written down before anyone was assessed. Consistency is the whole value: a rubric
  applied identically to everybody is harder to lobby than a panel.
* **Applies your value system, not ours.** Pillar 3 exists so a citizen can write down their own rules
  for deciding this versus that and have those applied at scale — to their standard, not the movement's.
* **Summarises a month, advisory only.** The monthly karma pass writes a narrative, notes standouts and
  integrity flags, and nominates history-book entries. **It never awards a point.**

## What it never does

It never approves anything, publishes anything, sets an award, or overrides a human. Every path from
an AI output to something the public reads passes through a person.

## The arithmetic is not the AI

Worth separating clearly: the awards are **published equations over recorded acts**. You can reproduce
them with a calculator. No model is in that loop. Where AI appears, it is reading material and checking
reasoning, and its outputs are inputs to a human decision.
