# What only lives on a party door

This door is narrower than the other four **on purpose**, because narrower is what makes it usable by
people who disagree with each other about most things. Here is the list, so nobody has to guess.

## The list

* **Partisan plans.** A plan for what a community wants the country to look like is a partisan
  artefact by definition. The plan on this door is the high-level one: making sure the best
  candidates are found and can stay working for citizens.
* **The partisan laws and [fix bills](/good_bills/overview/) a community wants added.** Each base has its own list of statutes
  it wants repealed and its own repairs it wants written.
* **The political-spectrum vote** that matches a citizen to candidates who fit where they sit. This
  only makes sense inside one party's own field.
* **The board of directors.** Governance belongs to the community it governs.
* **The yearly strategic conference.**
* **Trust-score voting.** A community can only vouch for people it has some way of knowing. The
  roll-up here is read only — see
  **[The read-only roll-up](../trust_scores/the_read_only_rollup.mdx)**.
* **Religious framing.** One door's own messaging carries it. No religion is named on this door or on
  the other three.

## What is here instead

* Capture that runs against both bases at once —
  **[Why A Non-Partisan Site](../non_partisan_site/overview.mdx)**.
* A venue where both bases can reach a decision Washington has not —
  **[The cross-party forum](../non_partisan_site/the_cross_party_forum.mdx)**.
* Meritocracy in its high-level form: candidates most dedicated to citizens, strongest at refusing to
  be co-opted — **[Meritocracy](../meritocracy/overview.mdx)** and
  **[Qualifications](../qualifications/overview.mdx)**.
* The shared record itself — the laws, the problems, the [voting records](/voting_records/overview/) and the awards, which are one
  record behind all five doors.

## Why the split is written down rather than left to taste

A door that quietly drifted into carrying its own partisan package would stop being neutral ground,
and there would be no cross-party venue left anywhere. Publishing the boundary is what lets a reader
hold us to it.
