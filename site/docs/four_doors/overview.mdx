# One App, Four Doors

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**There are five sites. Which one am I supposed to use?**
:::

Whichever one is yours. They all run the **same app** over the **same record**, and none of them can
show you a different set of facts about a politician than another one shows.

## One app, one record

Five addresses, one piece of software:

| Door | For | Address |
|---|---|---|
| **We The Citizens** | everyone; the cross-party door | [WeTheCitizens.io](https://WeTheCitizens.io) |
| **We The Citizens R** | Republicans | [WeCitizensR.com](https://wecitizensr.com) |
| **We The Citizens D** | Democrats | [WeCitizensD.com](https://wecitizensd.com) |
| **We The Citizens L** | Libertarians | [WeCitizensL.com](https://wecitizensl.com) |
| **We The Citizens S** | Democratic Socialists | [WeCitizensSocialism.com](https://wecitizenssocialism.com) |

The edition is decided by the address you arrived on. Not four products, not four deployments, not
four data sets — **one app rendered five ways**.

The consequence is the part worth holding on to: the awards are computed **once, party-blind**. No
edition re-weights anything, and no edition can present a friendlier version of a voting record to
its own audience. If a member of Congress earns a Monkey Award, they earn the same one behind every
door, including their own party's.

## What each door is for

The party doors are where a community does its own work: its own plans, its own laws, its own
priorities, in its own vocabulary. Each one is written from **that audience's own messaging** and
from nothing else.

**No door is written to mirror another.** They are not pairs, none is another's reflection, and a door
may carry areas the others do not. Four audiences with four sets of priorities produce four different
shapes, and that is the intended result rather than a defect to be reconciled.

## What lives only on a party door

This `.io` door is deliberately narrower. The following exist **only** on the `.com` doors:

* **Partisan plans**, and the partisan laws and fix-it bills a community wants added.
* **The political-spectrum vote** that matches a citizen to candidates who fit where they sit.
* **The board of directors.**
* **The yearly strategic conference.**
* **Trust-score voting.** The roll-up here is read only — see
  **[Trust Scores](../trust_scores/overview.mdx)**.
* **Religious framing**, on the one door whose own messaging carries it. No religion is named on this
  door or on the other three.

## Find your door

* **You identify with a party.** Go to its door and do the work there. That is where your plans, your
  laws and your trust votes live.
* **You do not, or the problem is bigger than a party.** Stay here. This door is for the capture that
  damages both bases at once, and for the cross-party decisions the formal apparatus in Washington
  has not made. See **[Why A Non-Partisan Site](../non_partisan_site/overview.mdx)**.
* **You want to see who is running in your seat.** Any door does this, and every door shows you the
  strongest challenger for that seat **who is not from your side** as well as the one who is. See
  **[Replacing Captured Incumbents](../replace_incumbents/overview.mdx)**.

## What is not in this area

The messaging of any single party door. That is written on that door, from that door's own messaging
file.
