# Find your door

## You identify with a party

Go to its door and do the work there. That is where your plans, the laws your community wants added,
the [fix bills](/good_bills/overview/) it cares about, the political-spectrum vote and your trust votes all live.

* **[We The Citizens R](../r/overview.mdx)** — Republicans, at
  [WeCitizensR.com](https://wecitizensr.com)
* **[We The Citizens D](../d/overview.mdx)** — Democrats, at
  [WeCitizensD.com](https://wecitizensd.com)
* **[We The Citizens L](../l/overview.mdx)** — Libertarians, at
  [WeCitizensL.com](https://wecitizensl.com)
* **[We The Citizens S](../s/overview.mdx)** — Democratic Socialists, at
  [WeCitizensSocialism.com](https://wecitizenssocialism.com)

Nothing is lost by going there. It is the same record, the same awards and the same arithmetic, with
your own community's work on top of it.

## You do not, or the problem is bigger than a party

Stay here. This door is for capture that damages both bases at once, and for the cross-party
decisions the formal apparatus in Washington has not made. See
**[Why A Non-Partisan Site](../non_partisan_site/overview.mdx)** and
**[What crosses both parties](../non_partisan_site/what_crosses_both_parties.mdx)**.

Plenty of citizens are here permanently and that is a complete answer, not a waiting room.

## You just want to see who is running in your seat

Any door does this, and every one of them shows you the strongest challenger for that seat who is
**not** from your side as well as the one who is. Start at
**[Find who represents you](../legacy_politicians/find_who_represents_you.mdx)**, then
**[The seat view](../replace_incumbents/the_seat_view.mdx)**.

## You want to run it yourself and join nothing

Also a complete answer. The product runs locally on your own machine, with no account, and the whole
record is a repository you can clone. See **[Get Started](../get_started/overview.mdx)** and
**[Open Data](../open_data/overview.mdx)**.
