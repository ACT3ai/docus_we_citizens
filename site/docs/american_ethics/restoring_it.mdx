# Restoring it

Everything else on this site is machinery in service of this. It is worth saying plainly what the
machinery is *for*, because a movement that forgets its purpose keeps its tools and loses the point.

## Why new occupants are not enough

Replace every member and leave the arrangement in place, and the arrangement produces the same
behaviour from the new people within a term or two. That is not cynicism about the new people; it is
what an incentive structure does. The seats matter, and they are necessary — see
**[Replacing Captured Incumbents](../replace_incumbents/overview.mdx)** — but they are the first half
of the job.

The second half is removing the thing that shaped the behaviour:

* **The statutes that made the capture legal** — **[Problem Laws](../problem_laws/overview.mdx)**.
* **The language that closes the gap**, as actual bill text —
  **[Fixing the Law](../fix_laws/overview.mdx)** and **[Fix Bills](../good_bills/overview.mdx)**.
* **A record of who blocked the repair**, because obstruction is a decision and belongs beside the
  votes — **[Good Laws](../good_laws/overview.mdx)**.

## The four things that have to be true afterwards

1. **A citizen can check any claim without permission** — the code and the record are public and
   cloneable. **[Open Data](../open_data/overview.mdx)**, **[Repos](../repos/overview.mdx)**.
2. **What is published about a person is true and computed in the open** — awards derived from the
   official record, with the arithmetic shown and the gaps named.
   **[Politicians](../politicians/overview.mdx)**.
3. **Better candidates are chosen on merit, in advance, by the community that votes for them** —
   **[Meritocracy](../meritocracy/overview.mdx)**, **[Qualifications](../qualifications/overview.mdx)**.
4. **Honest behaviour stops being the losing strategy.** This is the one that actually matters, and
   the other three exist to produce it.

## The citizen side of the same ledger

Restoration is not only something done to Washington. **[Values](../values/overview.mdx)** is where
the ethical standard gets written down in a form that can be applied consistently, and
**[Karma](../karma/overview.mdx)** is the record of citizens who did the work — hash-chained, so a
name that earned its way in cannot be quietly written back out.

## What a citizen does this week

**[Take Action](../take_action/overview.mdx)**. Call a representative, add a source to a record, vet
a challenger, recruit one, draft a fix bill. Each is logged with a link so somebody else can check
it. The scarce resource in this movement is action, not opinion.
