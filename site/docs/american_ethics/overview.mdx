# Great American Ethics

<CTA />

Count a captured decision in dollars and you have counted the smallest part of it. The larger cost is
what it **teaches** — that the rules are for other people, that the written standard and the real one
are different documents, and that the honest way of doing things is the naive way.

That lesson is expensive, it compounds, and no appropriation reverses it.

## The standard, and where it is written down

The American ethical standard is not a mood. It is written down, and it is checkable:

* **The founding documents**, and the plain-language promises in them.
* **The laws Congress writes for everyone else** — the clearest available statement of what Congress
  itself says is right.
* **The social contract as citizens actually hold it**, which is the yardstick this site measures
  against. See **[The Social Contract](../social_contract/overview.mdx)**.

When those three disagree with each other, the disagreement is the finding.

## How a hijacked Congress degrades it, act by act

The damage is not abstract. It shows up as specific, documented practices, each of which is recorded
as a named problem with its sources attached:

* **Exempting itself from its own laws** — the rule applies to the country and not to the body that
  wrote it.
* **Settlements paid and hidden** — a wrong is compensated with public money and the record of it is
  kept from the public that paid.
* **No penalty for stonewalling** — an agency declines to produce what it owes, and nothing happens
  to anyone.

Each one is individually survivable. Together they teach the country a rule about who the rules are
for. The files behind these are in **[Problems](../problems/overview.mdx)**, and the pattern of
records withheld without basis runs through
**[Exposing the Deep State](../deep_state/overview.mdx)**.

## What it costs a country

* Honest actors are outcompeted, and they notice.
* Citizens who follow the rules learn that following them was optional.
* Trust becomes a thing only naive people extend — which is exactly the condition capture needs to
  keep operating.

## Restoring the standard is the point of the whole movement

Everything else on this site is machinery in service of this: a record anyone can check, awards that
tell the truth about a [voting record](/voting_records/overview/), better candidates chosen on merit, and laws written to close
the gaps that let all of it happen. See **[Values](../values/overview.mdx)** for how the ethical
standard is captured in a form that can be applied consistently, and
**[Karma](../karma/overview.mdx)** for the citizen side of the same ledger.
