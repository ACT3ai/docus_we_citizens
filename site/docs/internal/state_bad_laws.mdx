# State Bad Laws

State-level legislation that restricts civil liberties, chills free speech or assembly, or expands government surveillance and detention power beyond constitutional limits.

## The List

See the full catalog with details: [State Bad Laws Overview](/docs/internal/state-bad-laws/overview)

* [Anti-BDS State Contractor Laws](/docs/internal/state-bad-laws/anti-bds-state-laws) — 38 states require contractors to certify they do not boycott Israel; critics call it government-coerced political speech.
* [Texas HB 89 — Anti-BDS (2017)](/docs/internal/state-bad-laws/texas-hb89-anti-bds) — Bars state contracts with Israel boycotters; tied to hurricane-aid pledge controversy.
* [Arkansas Act 710 — Anti-BDS (2017)](/docs/internal/state-bad-laws/arkansas-act710-anti-bds) — Contractor certification requirement upheld in 8th Circuit; SCOTUS declined review.
* [Florida SB 1678 — Anti-BDS Expansion (2025)](/docs/internal/state-bad-laws/florida-sb1678-anti-bds) — Extends anti-BDS rules to artists, nonprofits, and additional public funding streams.
* [Florida HB 1 — Anti-Riot Act (2021)](/docs/internal/state-bad-laws/florida-hb1-anti-riot) — Expanded riot definitions, felony protest charges, driver immunity for striking protesters.
* [Florida Stop WOKE Act (2022)](/docs/internal/state-bad-laws/florida-stop-woke-act) — Restricts how employers and schools discuss race and gender; partially blocked by courts.
* [Critical Infrastructure Anti-Protest Laws](/docs/internal/state-bad-laws/critical-infrastructure-anti-protest) — Felony-level penalties for protesting near pipelines, refineries, and utilities; passed in 20+ states since 2017.
* [Georgia Domestic Terrorism Expansion (2017)](/docs/internal/state-bad-laws/georgia-domestic-terrorism) — Expanded domestic-terror law used against Cop City protesters and climate activists.
