# Privacy Policy

**Status: placeholder.** This document is a working draft written so the product's sign-up flow has
a real destination to link to. It has **not** been reviewed by counsel. Replace it before the hosted
app takes public sign-ups.

**Last updated:** July 30, 2026

---

## 1. The short version

We The Citizens is **local-first**. Run it on your own computer and your positions, values, evidence
chains, and drafts stay in flat files on your own disk. We never see them. The hosted app at
app.WeTheCitizens.io stores what you choose to put there, and nothing more.

## 2. What we collect

**When you sign in with Google:** your name, email address, and profile picture, as supplied by
Google. We never receive your Google password.

**What you create in the hosted app:** the decisions, positions, values, evidence chains, problems,
and proposed fixes you save, and whether you chose to publish them.

**Operational logs:** requests to the server, with timestamps and error detail, kept so the service
can be run and debugged.

## 3. What we do not do

* We do not sell or rent your personal information.
* We do not run third-party advertising or analytics trackers on the app. The sign-up page makes no
  third-party requests at all — its fonts and images are served from our own origin.
* We do not read the local files of a copy of the app running on your own machine.
* We do not disclose who you are because your positions are unpopular.

## 4. Publishing is a choice

Nothing you write becomes visible to anyone else until you publish it. Publishing to a community or
to WeTheCitizens.social makes that content, and the identity attached to it, visible to others. That
step is deliberate, it is yours, and it cannot be silently undone by us.

## 5. How long we keep it

Your hosted account data is kept while your account exists. Ask us to delete the account and we
delete the personal data associated with it, except where we are required to keep records. Content
you published to a shared surface may persist where others have already received it.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your
personal information, and to object to certain processing. Write to us and we will honor it.

## 7. Security

Sessions are signed tokens; secrets are stored outside the code repository; access to production data
is limited to the people operating the service. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you
promptly if a breach affects you.

## 8. Children

The platform is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their
information.

## 9. Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and change the "last updated" date.

## 10. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: **privacy@wethecitizens.io**
