# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 17, 2026

---

## 1. The short version

We The Citizens is **local-first**. Run it on your own computer and your positions, values, evidence
chains, and drafts stay in flat files on your own disk. We never see them. The hosted app at
app.WeTheCitizens.io stores what you choose to put there, and nothing more.

Sign-in is Google. We never receive your password. We do not sell your personal information, we do
not run third-party ad or analytics trackers, and nothing you write is visible to anyone else until
you publish it.

## 2. Who is responsible for your data

We The Citizens is a project of ACT3ai, Inc., a Delaware corporation, doing business as
wethecitizens.io. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean ACT3ai, Inc. For readers in the EEA and
the UK, ACT3ai, Inc. is the data controller for the personal data described here.

This policy covers:

* the website at **wethecitizens.io**
* the hosted app at **app.WeTheCitizens.io**
* the API at **api.WeTheCitizens.io**
* the four party front doors at **WeCitizensR.com**, **WeCitizensD.com**, **WeCitizensL.com** and **WeCitizensSocialism.com**
* the paired social network at **WeCitizens.social**

It also explains what happens with the open-source copy of the app that you run yourself: almost
nothing, because that copy keeps your data on your own machine. See sections 7 and 13.

## 3. What we collect

**Account data, from Google.** When you sign in with Google, through OpenAuthFederated, we receive
your name, email address, profile picture, and a stable Google account identifier. We never receive
your Google password. We store a signed session token so you stay signed in.

**What you create in the hosted app.** The decisions, positions, values, evidence chains, problems,
and proposed fixes you save, along with the communities and areas you belong to and whether you chose
to publish each item.

**Operational logs.** Requests to the server and API, with timestamps, IP address, user agent, the
route requested, response status, and error detail. These exist so the service can be run, secured,
and debugged.

**Support correspondence.** If you email us, we keep the message, your address, and our reply, so we
can answer you and keep a record of what we agreed.

**What we do not collect.** We do not ask for a password, a payment card, a government identifier, a
precise location, biometrics, or health data. We do not collect the contents of a local install.

## 4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of first-party cookies and browser storage entries, and nothing else:

* A **session cookie or token** so you stay signed in. Without it you would sign in on every page.
* **Security and preference storage**, such as a CSRF token and your light or dark theme choice.

We do not set third-party advertising cookies, we do not run third-party analytics trackers, and we do
not participate in cross-site ad networks. The sign-up page makes no third-party requests at all: its
fonts and images are served from our own origin.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser. If you block the session cookie, sign-in will not
work.

## 5. How we use what we collect

We use personal data only for these purposes:

* To create your account and sign you in.
* To store, display, and sync the content you create in the hosted app.
* To publish content you have chosen to publish, to the community or network you chose.
* To operate, secure, monitor, and debug the Services, including preventing abuse, spam, and
  manufactured support.
* To answer your questions and provide support.
* To send service messages you need, such as a security notice, a policy change, or an account
  action. We will ask before sending anything promotional.
* To comply with the law, and to establish or defend legal claims.
* To understand aggregate usage of the movement's surfaces, using our own server logs, in aggregate
  form, not to build a profile of you.

We do not use your personal data to build advertising profiles, and we do not use the private content
in your account to train a model.

## 6. Legal bases for processing (EEA and UK readers)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on these legal bases under
the GDPR and UK GDPR:

* **Contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to give you the account and the app features you asked for.
* **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to keep the Services secure, prevent abuse, debug
  problems, and understand aggregate usage. We balance those interests against your rights, and you
  may object. See section 12.
* **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for anything optional, such as an email you asked to receive.
  You may withdraw consent at any time.
* **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c)) — to respond to a valid legal requirement.

Content you publish may reveal political opinions, which is a special category of data under Art. 9.
We process it only because you have made it public yourself through a deliberate publishing step,
which is the Art. 9(2)(e) basis, and, where required, on your explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a).
Publishing is always your choice. See section 8.

## 7. What we do not do

* We do not sell or rent your personal information.
* We do not run third-party advertising or analytics trackers on the app. The sign-up page makes no
  third-party requests at all — its fonts and images are served from our own origin.
* We do not read the local files of a copy of the app running on your own machine.
* We do not disclose who you are because your positions are unpopular.
* We do not hand over a citizen's identity to anyone who simply asks. See section 9.

## 8. Publishing is a choice

Nothing you write becomes visible to anyone else until you publish it. Publishing to a community or
to WeCitizens.social makes that content, and the identity attached to it, visible to others. That
step is deliberate, it is yours, and it cannot be silently undone by us.

Two consequences worth being clear about:

* Published content can be copied, quoted, indexed by search engines, and archived by people we do
  not control. Unpublishing removes it from our surfaces. It does not recall copies others already
  have.
* If you publish under your real name, your positions are attached to your real name. That is the
  point of the movement, and it is still a choice you make one item at a time.

## 9. Sharing and disclosure

We share personal data in four situations, and no others.

**Service providers.** Companies that run infrastructure for us, under contract, only to provide the
service, and never to use your data for their own purposes. Those are, in current form: our cloud
hosting and content-delivery providers, Google for sign-in, GitHub for source code and parts of the
public website, our email provider, and the AI provider that powers the app's AI features.

**WeCitizens.social**, and any community you join, for content you chose to publish there.

**Lawful process.** We may disclose data if we are required to by a valid subpoena, court order, or
other legal demand, or where disclosure is necessary to protect someone from serious harm. When we
receive one of these, we:

* Check that it is valid, and narrow it where it overreaches.
* Push back where the law lets us, including on demands aimed at a citizen's political speech.
* **Notify you before we disclose anything, wherever we are legally permitted to do so**, so you have
  a chance to object. If a gag order forbids notice, we will tell you as soon as the order allows.

**Business transfer.** If the project is merged, acquired, or reorganized, data may transfer to the
successor, which must honor this policy or give you notice and a choice before changing it.

## 10. International transfers

We operate from the United States, and our service providers are mostly in the United States. If you
use the Services from outside the United States, your personal data will be transferred to and
processed in the United States, where privacy law differs from your own.

For transfers from the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, we rely on the European Commission's Standard
Contractual Clauses, and the UK Addendum where it applies, in our contracts with service providers,
plus additional safeguards where appropriate. Write to privacy@wethecitizens.io for details of the
mechanism that covers a specific transfer.

## 11. How long we keep it

* **Account data** is kept while your account exists.
* **Content you created and did not publish** is deleted with the account.
* **Content you published to a shared surface** may persist where others have already received it,
  and in the public record of a community.
* **Operational logs** are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted or aggregated, except where a
  specific log is preserved for a security investigation or a legal hold.
* **Support correspondence** is kept for up to 24 months.
* **Backups** roll off on their own schedule, normally within 35 days of deletion.

Ask us to delete the account and we delete the personal data associated with it, except where we are
required to keep records or need them to resolve a dispute.

## 12. Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to:

* Tell you what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
* Correct data that is wrong.
* Delete your account and the personal data associated with it.
* Export what you created in a portable, machine-readable form.
* Object to, or ask us to limit, a particular use of your data.
* Withdraw a consent you gave.

Write to **privacy@wethecitizens.io**. We answer within 30 days, and we will tell you if we need
longer. We may need to verify that the request really comes from you, which normally means replying
from the email address on the account. We do not charge for this, and we will not treat you worse for
asking. You may use an authorized agent, who must show they are authorized.

### California residents (CCPA and CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know the categories and specific pieces of
personal information we collect, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of third parties we
disclose to; the right to delete; the right to correct; the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of
personal information; the right to limit the use of sensitive personal information; and the right not
to be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights. We will not deny you service, charge
you a different price, or give you a lower quality of service because you exercised a privacy right.

**We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral
advertising**, as those terms are defined in the CCPA as amended by the CPRA. We have not done so in
the preceding 12 months. Because there is no sale or sharing, there is no "Do Not Sell or Share My
Personal Information" mechanism to offer, and a Global Privacy Control signal has nothing to opt out
of. The categories we collect are listed in section 3, and the purposes in section 5.

### EEA and UK residents (GDPR and UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right of access (Art. 15), the right to rectification
(Art. 16), the right to erasure (Art. 17), the right to data portability (Art. 20), the right to
object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21), the right to restriction of processing
(Art. 18), and the right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority, or with
the UK Information Commissioner's Office. We would rather you told us first, at
privacy@wethecitizens.io, so we can fix it, but you do not have to.

## 13. Security

* Sessions are signed tokens. Secrets are stored outside the code repository.
* Traffic to the website, the app, and the API is served over HTTPS.
* Access to production data is limited to the people operating the service, and is logged.
* Local installs keep your data on your own disk, which is the strongest privacy protection we can
  offer. Securing that machine is up to you.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you promptly, and
we will notify regulators where the law requires it.

## 14. Children under 13

The Services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal
information. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, write to
privacy@wethecitizens.io and we will delete it. If you are between 13 and the age of majority where
you live, use the Services only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

## 15. Do Not Track

Browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal, and there is still no common standard for what a site must
do in response. We do not track you across other websites in the first place, so there is nothing for
the signal to change. We do not respond to it, and we do not need to.

## 16. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the policy changes, and we will change the "last updated" date.
Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect. We will not make a change that
retroactively reduces the protection of data we already hold without giving you notice and a choice.
