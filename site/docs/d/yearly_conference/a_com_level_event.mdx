# A .com-Level Event, Not a Non-Partisan One

The conference is not held at the non-partisan level.

* The conference is not held at the non-partisan level. The .io has no conference of its own.
* Each party door runs its own, for its own community, in its own language.
* Do not describe it as a movement-wide non-partisan event.
