# Partisan Laws and Bills

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Do you have actual legislation?**
:::

The .com doors are where partisan law work happens. The .io does not host it. This is where Democrat voters add and prioritize the laws they care about.

* Legislation here is generated from real problems citizens raise, not from a wish list.
* Prioritized by the community, by the same voting and collaboration as everything else.
* Partisan by design. That is the point of a party door.

## In this area

* [Adding the Laws This Community Cares About](./add_the_laws_you_care_about.mdx) — Democrat voters add and shape the laws that matter to them.
* [Bills That Matter Most to Democrats](./bills_that_matter_most.mdx) — A concrete bill is a better test of a candidate than a general statement.

## Where this sits

This is one area of [We The Citizens D](/docs/d/overview), the Democrat front door at [WeCitizensD.com](https://wecitizensd.com). Its mirror on the other side of the axis is [We The Citizens R](/docs/r/overview) — same area, same shape, party label swapped.
