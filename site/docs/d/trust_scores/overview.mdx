# Trust Scores

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Where do I cast a vote that counts on a candidate?**
:::

Here. Trust scores are **built and voted at the party level**, and this is one of the party doors.

* **What they measure:** whether a candidate really works for citizens, and how hard they are to co-opt. The scoring reflects this community's judgment of independence and merit.
* **Where your vote counts:** on this door. Cast it here and it counts.
* **The non-partisan .io shows a combined view** stitched together from all four doors, and it is read-only. Nobody votes there.

## What this community can move, and what it cannot

* **The award arithmetic is computed once, for everybody, and no door touches it.** No edition re-weights it, re-rounds it, or sets its own threshold — there is nowhere in the configuration to put one.
* **The same number means the same thing on every door.** An incumbent who has earned 71 percent of an award renders identically on all four. Only the audience it gets explained to changes.
* **Which means it runs on incumbents from your own party too.** That is the point of it, not a side effect.
* **Every result shows its work** — every row, every weight, every source, on the page.
* **Trust buys attention, never authority.** Money buys nothing at all here.
