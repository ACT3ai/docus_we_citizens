# How This Door Relates to the Other Three

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Is this a Democrat-only movement?**
:::

Four party doors, one app: **R** Republicans, **D** Democrats, **L** Libertarians, **S** Democratic Socialists.

* R and D mirror each other on the partisan axis. L and S mirror each other on the economic axis. This door is **D**, and its mirror is **R**.
* Every door respects the others. We are replacing captured incumbents, not the voters behind another door.
* Not a separate product, a separate movement, or a separate set of principles.

## In this area

* [One App, Four Doors](./one_app_four_doors.mdx) — WeCitizensD.com is edition D of the one We The Citizens app, recognized by hostname.
* [Sending Non-Partisan Visitors Here](./sending_visitors_here.mdx) — The .io's job is to send you to the door for the party you care about.
