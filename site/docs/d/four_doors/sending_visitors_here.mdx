# Sending Non-Partisan Visitors Here

The .io's job is to send you to the door for the party you care about.

* The .io encourages a visitor to go to the door for the party they care about.
* A visitor who is one of Democrat voters and arrives at the neutral lobby should be pointed to this door.
* Arriving here should immediately feel like **more**, not like a narrower version of the .io.
