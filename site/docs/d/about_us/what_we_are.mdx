# What We Are, and Why We Cannot Be Bought

A movement built to get concentrated money out of politics has to be built so that concentrated money cannot get into it. Otherwise it is just the next thing somebody buys.

## The lesson

An organization whose leadership answers to donors, sponsors, or a self-appointing board can be redirected the moment it becomes inconvenient to somebody with a checkbook — and it usually is redirected quietly, without a vote and without an announcement. An organization whose leadership answers to its members cannot.

That is why the structure is written down before the positions are.

## What that means in practice

* **A movement, not a bureaucracy.** Very lightweight by design.
* **Member-owned.** Every major decision flows up from the membership. The [board](/d/board_of_directors/) serves at the pleasure of the people, not the other way around.
* **Radically transparent.** All finances, all board votes, all major decisions — documented and published. Every dollar visible.
* **Uncapturable.** No single donor, corporation, or foreign interest may hold outsized influence. Grassroots funding from real Americans only. That is the same fight as [Citizens United](/d/replace_incumbents/fixing_citizens_united), applied to ourselves first.
* **Accountable.** An elected board with term limits, annual independent audits published in full, and any board member recallable by member vote.

## Why it belongs on this page

Because it is the same test we apply to a member of Congress. We exist to establish whether an incumbent is captured and to [replace the captured ones with challengers who are not](/d/replace_incumbents/). A movement that could not pass its own test would have nothing to say.
