# Who This Door Is For

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Is this site for me?**
:::

If you vote Democrat, yes. This is the room where this community does its own work — finding challengers, writing the laws it cares about, and judging the people who ask for its votes.

* **Who it is for.** Voters who vote Democrat.
* **Who it is not for.** A visitor who wants the neutral version goes to [WeTheCitizens.io](/) instead. The .io is the lobby. This is the room where the work happens.
* **The promise.** Your side builds its own package here, in your own language. Nobody hands it down to you.
* **What we are not.** No fundraising, no donations, no campaign coordination, and no endorsement of a party. We back people, and we publish why.

Everything on this door points at one thing: your own elected officials, what they actually voted for, and who could replace them.

## In this area

* [Democrat Voters, Not the Establishment](./democrat_voters_not_the_establishment.mdx) — We are talking to the voter, not to the officeholder and not to the party apparatus.
* [Why You Are Here and Not on the Non-Partisan Site](./why_here_not_the_io.mdx) — The .io is deliberately neutral and will not take your partisan issues on. Everything partisan lives here.

## Where this sits

This is one area of [We The Citizens D](/docs/d/overview), the Democrat front door at [WeCitizensD.com](https://wecitizensd.com). It is written for Democrat voters, and nothing on the other three doors decides what belongs here.
