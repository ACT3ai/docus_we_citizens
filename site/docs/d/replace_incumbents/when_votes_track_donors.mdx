# When the Votes Track the Donors

The trigger is the voting record.

* The trigger is the voting record: when an incumbent's votes track their donors instead of their constituents, this community backs a challenger.
* We exist to find and lift challengers. An incumbent who has stayed independent can be backed on the merits — but on the merits, never on the seat.
* No safe seats. No automatic re-endorsement. Every cycle, everybody is measured again.
