# Citizens United Gets Fixed by This System

Unlimited outside money only works if it buys officeholders.

* Citizens United is the money-in-politics problem this community has wanted fixed for a generation, and the incumbents who benefit from it will not fix it.
* Our fix is structural: surface challengers who are committed to overturning it, and measure every candidate against that commitment.
* Unlimited outside money only works if it buys officeholders. Candidates who cannot be bought are how the money stops working.
