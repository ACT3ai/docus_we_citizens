# Replacing Incumbents Controlled by Elites and Money in Politics

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Who exactly are you trying to replace, and how?**
:::

The core action of this door: get political challengers into power to replace incumbents who answer to elites and concentrated money instead of to the people who elected them.

* **Party label is not the qualification.** Independence from those forces is.
* **The trigger is the voting record**, not a personality or a primary-season quarrel.
* If you remember one sentence from this door, make it that first one.
