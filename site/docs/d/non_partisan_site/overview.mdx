# What Stays Up at the Non-Partisan Site

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**What is the difference between this site and the .io?**
:::

[WeTheCitizens.io](/) is the neutral lobby. This door is the room where your side does the work. Some things stay up there on purpose.

* **The .io has exactly one plan, at the top level:** find the best candidates who will keep working for citizens. It does not take on partisan issues, and it has no board of directors.
* **Everything partisan lives down here** — the plans, the laws, the bills, the board, the conference, and the trust scores you actually vote on.
* **The combined, cross-party trust view stays up there.** It is stitched together from all four doors and it is read-only. The voting that feeds it happens here.
* **No religion appears in either place.** That language belongs to one other door and to nowhere else.
* When something belongs to the .io, we point at it instead of restating it. Two copies of the same paragraph is how the two versions drift apart.
