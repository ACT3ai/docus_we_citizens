# Economics That Work for Workers

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**What does this do for my paycheck?**
:::

Economics is the center of this door, and the test for every economic plank is the same: **does it work for the worker.**

* **The ordinary economics of a working life:** wages, hours, healthcare, housing, childcare, debt. Not abstractions.
* **The rigged layer is what stands between a working economy and this community.** Removing it is the work in [Removing the Rigged Economy](/docs/d/rigged_economy).
* **Candidates get measured on whether their record actually moved those numbers** — not on whether they gave the speech.
* A plank with no worker-facing test does not belong on this door.
