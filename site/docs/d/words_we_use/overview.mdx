# Words We Use and Words We Avoid

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**How should I talk about this?**
:::

Language is the whole job of this door. Two audiences never share a page, and we never claim a status we do not hold.

* Get the party mapping right every time: **R** Republicans, **D** Democrats, **L** Libertarians, **S** Democratic Socialists.
* Never claim a status, an endorsement, a funding source or an outcome we do not have.
* If a phrase from another door shows up in copy here, it was pasted in by mistake. Take it out.

## In this area

* [Words We Use](./words_we_use.mdx) — The phrases that belong on this door.
* [Words We Avoid](./words_we_avoid.mdx) — The phrases that never appear on this door.
