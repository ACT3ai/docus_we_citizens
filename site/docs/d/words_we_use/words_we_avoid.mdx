# Words We Avoid

The phrases that never appear on this door.

* **Replacement for TPUSA.** Republican door only.
* **The political right**, **voters who vote Republican**, and anything else addressed to the Republican audience.
* **Christian values**, and any single religion.
* The non-partisan voice, including calling this site a nonpartisan one. That copy lives on the .io.
* Attacks on the voters behind the R, L or S doors. Their captured incumbents are fair game; the voters are not.
* Any promised political outcome and any claimed endorsement.
