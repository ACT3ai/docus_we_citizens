# Dispute a record

## Self-interest is labelled, never blocked

A politician correcting their own record is **exactly the correction the movement wants**. The person
with the strongest incentive to find an error is also the person most likely to find one.

So a submission from the subject is **flagged** as self-interested — visibly, to reviewers — and then
reviewed on its merits. The movement does not refuse information because of who it came from; it
refuses to hide who it came from.

## Staff can file on your behalf

Delegation is filable on day one. It renders **unverified** until an admin confirms it, and the
correction is reviewed in the meantime.

Requiring the paperwork before accepting a correction would mean publishing something wrong for longer
than necessary, which is a strange thing for an accuracy process to do.

## What to file

**Point at the row.** *"This vote is recorded as a yes; the roll call shows a no"* is actionable in
minutes.

**Cite the public record.** A citation closes the question without anybody weighing your word against
ours.

**Separate the arithmetic from the inputs.** If a vote is recorded wrongly, that is a correction. If
you think the *weights* are wrong, that is a different and entirely fair argument — and the right place
to make it is against the published equations, in public, where everybody can see it.

## What you get back

Approved, sent back for changes, or declined. The last two carry a **mandatory stated reason**, shown
to you in the reviewer's own words. Nothing is silently dropped, and nothing is hard-deleted — what the
movement was asked and chose not to do is part of its record too.

## The posture that binds every page about you

* **State what somebody did or said**, with a citation — never what they are or what they wanted.
* **No claim about a living person without a source.**
* **An unreviewed submission is never rendered publicly.**
* **An absence is never rendered as a finding** — the gray-brown flamingo means *not assessed*, and
  must never read as a judgment.
* **The percentage is of an award**, and the page says so in words.

## What the channel is not

It is not a route to remove an accurate record, and it is not a negotiation over the arithmetic. Both
the acts and the equations are public, which is what makes the whole thing arguable in the first place.
