# Politicians

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Are you just calling people names with extra steps?**
:::

This area is the doctrine. The rosters and the awards each have their own areas; what is here governs
all of them.

## Rule 1 — it is an award, never a score

**Everything this product publishes about a politician is an award, or the absence of one.** A person
is either given an award, a percentage of one, or nothing at all.

*"The movement gave this voting record a 71% Monkey Award"* is a statement about a record, computed by
a published equation, that the subject can check line by line.

*"This person is a 71"* is a grade on a human being. The product does not say it, and the language on
every page holds that line — a page reads **Awards for**, never *Scores for*; a record with no computed
number reads **No award yet**, never *Not yet scored*.

## Rule 2 — the wall

**The in-office voting record — votes, authorship, and instruments blocked from advancing — is the only
thing that feeds the [Monkey](../monkey/overview.mdx) and [Llama](../llama/overview.mdx) awards.**

Everything else the movement knows about somebody — interviews, transcripts, background, resume,
education, public statements, qualification assessments — **never** bubbles up into those. It feeds the
[Flamingo](../flamingo/overview.mdx).

The wall runs both directions and is enforced in the build, not remembered.

A person can hold both kinds at once, and both render side by side: an award percentage on one side, a
flamingo state on the other. Neither contaminates the other.

## Rule 3 — an absence is never a finding

The Flamingo's gray-brown state means *we have not assessed this person*. It must never be rendered as,
sorted as, or collapsed into *we assessed them and found nothing good*.
