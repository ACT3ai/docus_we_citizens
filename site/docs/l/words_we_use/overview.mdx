# Words We Use and Words We Avoid

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**How should I talk about this?**
:::

This door has a vocabulary, and it is public on purpose. If you are going to carry the argument to somebody else, these are the words it is built out of.

* **The words we use** are the ones this community already uses: libertarians, the liberty movement, political challengers, incumbents controlled by globalist elites, meritocracy, the will of the people, narrative control, works for citizens, cannot be co-opted, the rigged economy, an ethical and clean economy, ideal for workers. Plus the three that are ours alone — the non-aggression principle, consent, and self-ownership.
* **The words we avoid** start with attacks on the voters behind the Republican, Democrat and Democratic Socialist doors. Their captured incumbents are fair game. Their voters are not.
* **Nothing we write compares the parties or assigns a motive to one.** The unit of judgement is a person and a vote.
* **We never claim a status, an endorsement, a funding source or an outcome we do not have.** No promised election result, and no backer we do not actually have.
* **Get the mapping right every time:** **R** Republicans, **D** Democrats, **L** Libertarians, **S** Democratic Socialists.

Two audiences never share a page. Copy written for the non-partisan .io, or for another door, does not belong on this one — and if it turns up here, it was pasted in by mistake.
