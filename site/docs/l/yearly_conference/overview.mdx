# Yearly Conference on Liberty Strategy

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Do you hold events?**
:::

A yearly strategic conference for this community. It is a .com-level event, not a non-partisan one, and each party door runs its own.

* It is where the community's yearly direction gets set together.
* The speaker list is the test — see below.
* Do not describe it as a movement-wide non-partisan event. It is not one.

## In this area

* [Speakers Who Represent Citizens](./speakers_who_represent_citizens.mdx) — The speaker list is the test.

## Where this sits

This is one area of [We The Citizens L](/docs/l/overview), the Libertarian front door at [WeCitizensL.com](https://wecitizensl.com). Its mirror on the other side of the axis is [We The Citizens S](/docs/s/overview) — same area, same shape, party label swapped.
