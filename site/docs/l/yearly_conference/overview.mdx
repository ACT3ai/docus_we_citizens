# Yearly Conference on Liberty Strategy

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Do you hold events?**
:::

Once a year this community meets and sets its own direction for the year ahead.

* **The direction gets set there, together.** It does not get announced there.
* **The speaker list is the test:** people who represent citizens, never people put on a stage by globalist elites or by the donor class. A conference the donor class can buy a slot at is a conference the donor class owns.
* **No money moves through it.** No fundraising, no donations, no campaign coordination. That is what makes the speaker-list test something we can be held to rather than something we say.
* **It is this door's event.** Each party door runs its own, at the .com level. It is not a movement-wide non-partisan event, and we do not describe it as one.
