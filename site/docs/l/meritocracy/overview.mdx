# Meritocracy: Finding the Best Candidates

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**How do you decide who is 'best' without just picking favorites?**
:::

Meritocracy is the engine underneath everything on this platform: find the candidates most dedicated to always work for citizens, and strongest at pushing off being co-opted.

* Shared with the .io and with all four doors. The engine does not change when the door does.
* What changes here is the input: on this door, meritocracy runs on top of the liberty movement's own priorities rather than on a neutral baseline.
* The arithmetic that turns this into a number lives in [Trust Scores](/docs/l/trust_scores).

## In this area

* [Dedicated to Always Work for Citizens](./dedicated_to_citizens.mdx) — The test is what happens after the election, not what was promised before it.
* [Strong Enough to Refuse Being Co-Opted](./strong_enough_to_refuse.mdx) — Being good is not enough if the pressure turns you.
* [Matching Candidates to Where You Sit on the Spectrum](./matching_the_spectrum.mdx) — Libertarians are not one position, so we do not flatten them into one.

## Where this sits

This is one area of [We The Citizens L](/docs/l/overview), the Libertarian front door at [WeCitizensL.com](https://wecitizensl.com). Its mirror on the other side of the axis is [We The Citizens S](/docs/s/overview) — same area, same shape, party label swapped.
