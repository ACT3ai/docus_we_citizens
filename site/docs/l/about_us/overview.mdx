# About Us for Libertarian Voters

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Is this site for me?**
:::

If you are a libertarian, yes. This is the room where the liberty movement does its own work — finding challengers, naming the laws it wants passed and repealed, and judging the people who ask for its votes.

* **Who it is for.** Libertarians, and the liberty movement more broadly.
* **Who it is not for.** A visitor who wants the neutral version goes to [WeTheCitizens.io](/) instead. The .io is the lobby. This is the room where the work happens.
* **The promise.** Your side builds its own package here, in your own language. Nobody hands it down to you.
* **You do not have to join anything to use it.** The app runs on your own computer. Reading, reasoning, recording your positions and running the checks all work with no account and no server — nothing leaves your machine unless you decide it does.
* **What we are not.** No fundraising, no donations, no campaign coordination, and no endorsement of a party. We back people, and we publish why.

Everything on this door points at one thing: your own elected officials, what they actually voted for, and who could replace them.

* [Libertarian Voters, Not the DC Swamp](./libertarian_voters_not_the_swamp.mdx) — We are talking to the voter, not to the officeholder and not to the party apparatus.

## Where this sits

This is one area of [We The Citizens L](/docs/l/overview), the Libertarian front door at [WeCitizensL.com](https://wecitizensl.com). It is written for libertarians, and nothing on the other three doors decides what belongs here.
