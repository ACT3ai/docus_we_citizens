# Why You Are Here and Not on the Non-Partisan Site

The .io is deliberately neutral and will not take your partisan issues on. Everything partisan lives here.

* The neutral lobby at [WeTheCitizens.io](/) is intentionally neutral. It will not take your partisan issues on, and that is by design rather than by omission.
* Everything partisan lives on this door: the plans, the laws, the bills, the board, the conference, and the trust scores you actually vote on.
* The .io's job is to send you here. This door's job is to be worth arriving at.
