# Verifiable Meritocracy Instead of Narrative Control

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**How would I check that this is really a meritocracy?**
:::

The slot this door fills: a place where you can **verify** that the best new political leaders are being surfaced on merit and on the will of the people.

* The alternative we are displacing is narrative control — foreign services, intelligence services and concentrated money deciding in advance who gets elected.
* You do not have to take our word for any of it. Every layer is open to inspection — see [Open Source, Open Data, Open AI](/docs/l/open_everything).
* Verifiable is the operative word. An unverifiable meritocracy is just a different small group picking.
