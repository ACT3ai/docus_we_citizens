# Verifiable Meritocracy Instead of Narrative Control

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**How would I check that this is really a meritocracy?**
:::

By checking it yourself. That is the whole point of this door: a place where the best new political leaders get surfaced on merit and on the will of the people, and where you can go confirm that is what happened.

* **The alternative we are displacing is narrative control** — foreign services, intelligence services and concentrated money deciding in advance who gets elected.
* **Verifiable is the operative word.** An unverifiable meritocracy is a different small group picking, with better branding.

## What checking it actually looks like

* **Redo the arithmetic.** Every result about a candidate is a published calculation over public records. The numerator, the denominator and every row are on the page. A citizen with a calculator can reproduce a published claim about a named living person.
* **Read the code and the data.** Both are open. See [Open Source, Open Data, Open AI](/docs/l/open_everything).
* **Check who approved what.** The approved data sits in a public repository, and its commit history is the approval record — what was decided and when. There is no separate approvals database behind it.
* **Read what the system cannot do.** It cannot decide anything by AI. It cannot rank one person by comparison to another. It cannot let money buy standing. Those are stated limits, not promises.
