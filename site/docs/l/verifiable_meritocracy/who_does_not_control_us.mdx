# Who Does Not Fund Us and Who Does Not Control Us

Independence is a claim that has to keep being earned, so we state what we do not take.

* Not the donor class. Not globalist elites. Not a foreign government, and not an intelligence service running narrative control.
* Independence is not a status you announce once. It is a claim you keep earning, which is why the honest version of it is a list of what we do **not** take.
* We never claim a status, a funding structure or an endorsement we do not actually hold.
