# Foreign Intelligence: No Puppet Master Over US Citizens

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Is somebody outside the country picking my representatives?**
:::

A foreign service steering US politics is the purest form of the thing this door exists to stop, and it is the thing the liberty movement has warned about longest. A vote that somebody else already decided is not an election.

* **It does not arrive with a flag on it.** It shows up as narrative control over who gets elected — funding, placed media, coordinated accounts, captured staff.
* **Foreign leverage on an officeholder is disqualifying.** Full stop.
* **The answer is exposure, plus challengers who cannot be bought.** We document the mechanism and name it in public. You get the receipts and judge for yourself.

Our own services are the [previous area](/docs/l/us_intelligence). This one is about services that are not ours at all.

## What we can show, and what we cannot

* **We name the category we can prove, not the person we cannot.** Saying it about a named person takes the record, and we hold ourselves to that.
* **Coordinated inauthentic narrative control is treated as exactly that**, wherever it comes from, including from people we would otherwise agree with.
* **What is missing gets marked as missing.** We would rather publish 14 of 17 links and name the 3 we cannot get than hand you something that only looks complete.
