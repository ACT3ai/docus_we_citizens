# US Intelligence Answers to US Citizens

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Can an agency decide my election?**
:::

Not if it answers to citizens. US intelligence services answer to US citizens, or they are not ours.

* **The failure mode** is a service taking direction from non-citizens, from foreign money, or from a private power that never stood for election.
* **Leverage over officeholders is how capture actually works.** Collected files, selective leaks, and briefings used as pressure all count.
* **Oversight you can verify**, not oversight you are told exists.
* **Candidates get measured on whether they will impose that control**, not on whether they say they support it.

Foreign services are the [next area](/docs/l/foreign_intelligence). This one is about our own.

## What can actually be checked

* **Every claim traces to the official record** — the House Clerk, the Senate, congress.gov, govinfo, a state's own site. Not a news write-up and not a summary site.
* **The vote and our reading of it are two separate columns.** "Voted yea" is a public fact with a citation next to it. "This one went against citizens" is our reading, and it is labelled as ours.
* **Where there is no member-level record at all** — a voice vote, unanimous consent, a committee action taken without objection — that gets written down as a gap rather than skipped. "No member is on record on this provision" is itself the finding.
