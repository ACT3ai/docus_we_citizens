# No Agency Decides an Election

No agency should be able to decide an election outcome, quietly or loudly.

* No agency should be able to decide an election outcome, quietly or loudly.
* Operations aimed at US voters are the line, whatever they are labeled internally.
* Oversight a citizen can verify, not oversight we are told exists.
