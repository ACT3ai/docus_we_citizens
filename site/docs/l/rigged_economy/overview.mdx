# Keep Ethical Economy. Remove Rigged Layer

We do not hand you an economic plan. Libertarians write this one, in their own language, and this is where they write it.

Start with what we are not touching. **The healthy economy is the part that works** — voluntary exchange, real risk, real reward. That stays, and it is the thing being defended here. What comes off is the layer standing where a market should be.

* **The means:** remove the rigged layer — the subsidies, the carve-outs, the regulatory capture, the bailouts. That layer is not a market. It is what stands where a market should be, and every piece of it was written by somebody who benefits from it.
* **The end:** an economy that is ethical and clean, where a business wins by being better rather than by being written into a bill.
* **The test:** it has to be ideal for workers too. That is stated here, on this door, and it is not a concession to anybody.

Every community names its own layer. Libertarians may name rules nobody on another door would put first, and that is expected — nobody on another door decides which rules belong on this list.

And the reason it has never come off is not the other party. A captured Congress does not repeal what captured it, whoever drafted the repeal. So the step before this package is [replacing captured incumbents with challengers strong enough to stay uncaptured](/docs/l/replace_incumbents) — after that, this package has somewhere to go.

## How a complaint becomes a bill

* **A problem has to be fixable to count.** Every problem carries a statement of what the world looks like once it is fixed. A problem with no statable fixed state is a complaint, and it does not go in.
* **Then it gets traced to the instrument.** Which enacted law carries the carve-out, and who voted it in.
* **Then the fix gets written.** Legislative language a member could file tomorrow — often a repeal — built from what citizens actually voted for rather than from what a staffer drafted.
