# Open AI

Open models where we can, open prompts and open method always.

* The AI that reasons over positions, evidence and candidates is open: open models where we can, open prompts and open method always.
* A citizen can see why the AI concluded what it concluded, and disagree with it.
* AI advises citizens here. It never decides for them.
