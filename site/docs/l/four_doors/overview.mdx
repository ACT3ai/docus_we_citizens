# How This Door Relates to the Other Three

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Is this a Libertarian-only movement?**
:::

No. There are four party doors on one app: **R** Republicans, **D** Democrats, **L** Libertarians, **S** Democratic Socialists. This is L.

* **Each door belongs to its own community.** This one is written for libertarians and is free to differ from the other three in any way. There is no pairing and no mirror between them.
* **Every door respects the others.** We are replacing captured incumbents. We are not going after the voters behind another door.
* **One app, not four.** Same software and the same data behind all of them. Which door you get is decided by the address you came in on. WeCitizensL.com is edition L of the one We The Citizens app.
* **The other side's best challenger is always on the page.** Every seat shows the incumbent, the best challenger from your side, and the strongest challenger from any other party — same size, same arithmetic. A door that only ever showed its own team would be a campaign tool, and we are not building one.
