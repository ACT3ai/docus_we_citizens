# No China-Style Social Credit in America

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Could a social credit system happen here?**
:::

A social credit system — a score that decides what a citizen is allowed to do — never gets built in America.

* **That includes the private-sector versions of it:** debanking, de-platforming as punishment, and scoring citizens by political behavior.
* **Candidates get measured on whether they will outlaw it before it arrives**, not after. After is too late; the infrastructure is the hard part and it does not get dismantled.
* **This does not describe the movement's own trust scores.** Those are about candidates asking you for power. They never measure citizens. See [Trust Scores](/docs/l/trust_scores).

## Why our own system cannot turn into one

* **What you believe never reaches what you are allowed to do.** Your political placement is walled off from your account standing and from every award. That is a one-way rule in the software, not a promise we are asking you to take on faith.
* **The party question is not a gate.** No feature is denied on it, no page is hidden behind it, you can change it whenever you want, and changing it does not wipe anything you already did.
* **You never have to have a name.** The app runs on your own machine, and reading, reasoning, recording your positions and running the checks all work with no account at all. A pseudonym can reach the top of the ladder. A system that punished anonymity would push exactly the citizens most at risk into either danger or irrelevance.
* **If you say an action was not lawful, or you are not sure, the app sends nothing** — no request, no draft, no log line, and the answer is never stored. A movement that kept a file of its members' self-incrimination would have built the evidence locker for the people it is opposing.
