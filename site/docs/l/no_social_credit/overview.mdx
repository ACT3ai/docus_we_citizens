# No China-Style Social Credit in America

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Could a social credit system happen here?**
:::

A social credit system — a score that decides what a citizen is allowed to do — never gets built in America.

* **That includes the private-sector versions of it:** debanking, de-platforming as punishment, and scoring citizens by political behavior.
* **Candidates get measured on whether they will outlaw it before it arrives**, not after. After is too late; the infrastructure is the hard part and it does not get dismantled.
* **This does not describe the movement's own trust scores.** Those are about candidates asking for power, never about citizens. See [Trust Scores](/docs/l/trust_scores).
