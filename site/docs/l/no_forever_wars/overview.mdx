# No Needless or Forever Wars, and No Draft

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Where do you stand on war?**
:::

Wars that serve globalist elites and not citizens are exactly the thing this door opposes.

* **Forever wars are a symptom of captured incumbents.** That is why the plank lives on this door and not in a foreign-policy annex.
* **No draft.** Conscription is the state claiming ownership of a person, and this door says no to it without qualification.
* **Candidates get measured on their actual war votes**, not their speeches. Authorizations, appropriations, and the votes nobody expected to be read back to them.
* This is the plank, not a full foreign-policy doctrine. It is deliberately narrow so it can be checked.
