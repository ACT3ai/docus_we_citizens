# Replacing Incumbents Controlled by Globalist Elites

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Who exactly are you trying to replace, and how?**
:::

The core action of this door: get political challengers into power to replace incumbents that are controlled by globalist elites and the Deep State.

* **Elections are the mechanism.** We beat captured incumbents at the ballot box — in the primary first, then in the general.
* **Incumbency earns nothing by itself.** No safe seats and no automatic re-endorsement. An incumbent who has stayed independent can be backed on the merits, like anybody else.
* **Party label is not the qualification. Independence from those forces is.** The swamp keeps re-electing captured incumbents under every letter, and saying that out loud is what separates us from a party organ.

If you carry one sentence out of this door, carry that last one.

## How a seat actually changes hands

* **You are shown the seat, not the personality.** Each seat carries three people side by side: the incumbent, the best challenger from your own side, and the strongest challenger from any other party — same size photo, same arithmetic. People leave. The seat is the thing you vote on.
* **Every claim traces to the official record** — the House Clerk, the Senate, congress.gov, govinfo, the FEC, a state's own site. Not a news write-up and not a summary site. An unsourced row gets dropped, and the drop gets reported.
* **Blocking a bill counts as the heaviest act there is.** A member who votes against a repeal took a position in public and can be answered for it at an election. A member who made sure the vote never happened erased the record itself — and erased every other member's chance to be counted.
* **No result depends on any other politician.** Nobody is graded on a curve against a cohort. A recompute of one person is a recompute of one person.
