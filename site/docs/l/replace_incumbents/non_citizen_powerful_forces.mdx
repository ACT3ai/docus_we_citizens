# Non-Citizen Powerful Forces, Domestic and Foreign

The threat is any powerful force that is not the citizen.

* Globalist elites, foreign governments, intelligence services and concentrated money — the common feature is that none of them is the citizen.
* The specific thing we look for is whether a group is running narrative control over who gets elected.
* We name the category, not individual people we cannot substantiate. A category we can prove beats a person we cannot.
