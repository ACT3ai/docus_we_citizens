# Community-Based Voting and Collaboration

:::tip[The question this area answers]
**Who decides what this community stands for?**
:::

You do. Positions, plans and candidate judgments come from libertarians voting and working together — not from a platform written upstairs and delivered to the membership.

* **The community builds the package.** Collaboration produces it, and the package is the product.
* **You vote where you sit on the spectrum.** That position drives which candidates get matched to you, and it shows the real spread inside this community — so a loud minority does not get mistaken for the whole.
* **Our job is the process.** Run it honestly, and keep it hard to capture. That is the whole of what we do here.

## What keeps this from being gamed

* **Your party answer only decides who you get compared against.** It is not a gate. No feature is denied on it, no page is hidden behind it, and it never becomes public unless you make it public.
* **Arriving on this domain does not answer it for you.** Libertarian comes pre-selected, with "you came in through the Libertarian door — is that right?", and you still have to click. Filling it in from the address would make the whole measurement circular.
* **Weight over other people has to be earned; nothing you do alone does.** A place where citizens document what their own officials did is a target for astroturf and sockpuppet accounts, so publishing into shared space and being counted in a community result are earned. Reading, reasoning and recording your own positions are not, and never will be.
