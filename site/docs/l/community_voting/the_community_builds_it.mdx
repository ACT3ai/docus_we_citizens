# The Community Builds the Package, We Do Not Hand It Down

Collaboration produces the package, and the package is the product.

* No top-down platform delivered to members. That model is exactly what this movement exists to replace.
* Collaboration produces the package, and the package is the product.
* Our job is to run the process honestly and keep it hard to capture. A capturable process produces a captured package.
