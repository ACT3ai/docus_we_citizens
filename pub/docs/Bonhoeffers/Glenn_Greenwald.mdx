## Notes

Lead reporter on the Edward Snowden NSA disclosures (2013) — among the clearest publicly documented cases of a journalist taking on US intelligence services at substantial personal and legal cost. Co-founded The Intercept; left in 2020 after an editorial dispute over his Hunter Biden reporting and has been independent since. Long-running, sustained, evidence-cited critic of the Israel lobby and US–Israel policy.
