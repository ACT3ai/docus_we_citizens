## Notes

Born 1989 in Los Angeles; raised by his mother and Cuban grandparents who fled Castro's Cuba in the 1960s. Built a music-video and commercial directing career in LA before relocating his family to Tennessee in 2019. Ran for the Republican nomination in Tennessee's 5th congressional district in 2022; the Tennessee Republican Party removed him from the primary ballot — a decision upheld by the Tennessee Supreme Court — and he then ran unsuccessfully as a write-in. As of 2025 has reportedly considered a renewed congressional bid in Tennessee.

Since mid-2024 has run a sustained, high-visibility campaign on X against corporate DEI programs. Several major brands — Tractor Supply, John Deere, Harley-Davidson, Brown-Forman, Lowe's, and Ford — publicly rolled back DEI initiatives after his campaigns highlighted them. Directed and narrated the 2024 documentary *The War on Children*. Persistence under sustained corporate and media pressure (criterion 4) and a large independent social-media platform (criterion 14) are the clearest qualifying traits. Public record on US intelligence-service abuses, foreign intelligence influence, and Israel-related criticism is thinner than the corporate-DEI track; those criteria are marked unclear rather than scored.
