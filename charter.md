# We The Citizens Charter


## Vision

Say that the way this will work is a very, very lightweight organization. It will operate the way Charlie Kirk wanted TP USA to ideally work.

It will have We The Citizens conference. That will be a once-a-year conference and have the thought leaders across the political spectrum that Charlie Kirk would have wanted. They will give talks. The board of directors will decide on who that is.

The second charter we will do is debates on college campuses. We'll encourage a list of people that can be great thought leaders politically. One person can give a visit to a university and can answer questions and debate. We want this to be able to progress great ideas and then also build future political leaders. We will repurpose this for content for social media so that way we can follow the good ethics.


The political charter of this is the following:
#1 Focus on good ethics.
Often, politically, things are betraying ethics for other agendas. We want this to be focused on good ethics.

#2 US politicians should work for US citizens. That means US politicians are not sellouts for money in politics, or the deep state, or the powerful that are domestic or foreign that are pursuing agendas that are against the interests and wants of the U.S. citizen based

#3 The structure of the deep state must be fully exposed. A plan is to fix any wrongs in that process. That's true for any domestic or foreign deep state that encompasses elites or any other puppet master equivalent power structure.

#4 a Christian organization. It exists to represent Christian values. Not any other religion's goals that may be at odds with US citizens or Christian values.

#5 Highly leveraged online political thought leadership that results in politically changing the most critical biggest problems we face.  This means harnessing online content, political debates, thought leadership towards the goals of getting in political leaders who will be able to solve our biggest problems. They will be able to have good people get into power, even if they have less money than their opponents who are sold out or controlled by the deep state.

#6 enable political candidates to win even when they have less money. Make a systemic process that makes the best candidate aligned with a citizen base win in the face that they have a lot less money in their campaign than their opposition, who have far more money. Create the infrastructure to enable this in a way that's run hyper efficiently financially and it has high leverage to deliver success.

#7 Permanently prevent voter fraud. When voting software systems in the process are proprietary and not 100% thoroughly policed by technology and transparency, then voter fraud will always happen. It will enable the deep state to have an avenue for voter fraud to control their politicians. Make happen open source software and hyper-policed transparency and security in the entire voting process to guarantee that only one qualified citizen can vote once and never more, and there can never be fraud in voting.


## Origin

Charlie Kirk built Turning Point USA into the largest conservative youth organization in America. He believed in empowering young Americans to take ownership of their communities, their campuses, and their government. When Charlie was taken from us on September 10, 2025, the organization he built was immediately captured by interests he would never have endorsed.

We The Citizens is the movement Charlie would have wanted. A very lightweight organization that operates the way he wanted TPUSA to ideally work. An organization where the board answers to the members, where financial transparency is non-negotiable, and where no corporate sponsor or foreign interest can buy influence over the mission.


## Programs

### We The Citizens Conference

A once-a-year national conference bringing together thought leaders across the political spectrum that Charlie Kirk would have wanted. The board of directors selects speakers. The conference is designed to advance the seven charter principles through public discourse, education, and coalition-building.

### Campus Debates

Debates on college campuses where great political thought leaders visit universities, answer questions, and engage students in debate. This program exists to progress great ideas and build future political leaders. All campus content is repurposed for social media to extend its reach in alignment with good ethics.

## Governance

### Member Sovereignty

The members own this organization. Every major decision — from leadership elections to policy positions — flows from the membership. The board of directors serves at the pleasure of the members.

### Radical Transparency

All finances are public. All board votes are public. All major decisions are documented and published on wethecitizens.io. If the people who fund and support this movement cannot see exactly where every dollar goes, the system is broken.

### No Corporate Capture

No single donor, corporation, or foreign interest may hold outsized influence over We The Citizens' direction. Funding comes from the grassroots — small-dollar donations from real Americans who believe in the mission.

### Accountable Leadership

The board of directors is elected by members on regular cycles. Board members have term limits. Financial audits are conducted annually by independent third parties and published in full. Any board member can be recalled by member vote.

### Digital-First Transparency

Every financial transaction, every board vote, every policy decision is published on wethecitizens.io. Members have real-time access to the state of their organization. This is not a feature — it is a requirement of the charter.


## The Promise

We The Citizens will never be captured. The charter is designed to make capture structurally impossible. When the members own the organization, when finances are transparent, when leadership is accountable and term-limited, and when no single interest can buy influence — the movement belongs to the people. That is what Charlie Kirk fought for. That is what we will protect.
